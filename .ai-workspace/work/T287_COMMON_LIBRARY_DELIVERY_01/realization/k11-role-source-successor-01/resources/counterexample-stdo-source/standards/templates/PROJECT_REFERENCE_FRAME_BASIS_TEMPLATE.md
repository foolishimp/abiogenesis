## Activation And Return Topology

Bind the sufficient direct Writer path and the material conditions requiring
coordination under the selected law. Direct entry still needs exact work
authority, admission, operation scope and closure conditions. It does not add
a ticket, coordinating appointment or review when none is applicable.

For coordinated work:

```text
existing authority
  -> Executive basis and outcome lock
  -> exact Worker, Reviewer, or specialist activation
  -> closed result and Reviewer technical triage where applicable
  -> Executive or declared conjunction
  -> Executive priority and boundary effect
  -> authorized disposition and next activation | stop | re-entry
```

No child result, tool output, model response, shared actor, or lateral handoff
activates another frame or authorizes continuation by itself
(`ACTIVATION.md` section 1, Admission).

Executive performs no file or Git mutation. The same actor may construct only
after entering a separately bound Writer activation with the inherited exact
grant. Changing roles does not create authority or independent assessment.

- exact coverage tuple and evaluation interval:

- intentional overlap and assurance-independence conditions:
- uncovered, conditional, conflicted, unknown, excluded, and out-of-frame

Coverage is complete only when every mandatory material evaluation and
interaction is closed, or governing authority explicitly retains its residual
consistently with the claim (`REFERENCE_FRAME.md` section 8, Operational full
coverage). Record each material row's status in the values of that section's
Coverage ledger. A `residual` row is
`excluded_by_authority` where governing authority explicitly retains its
residual consistently with the claim, and otherwise `conditionally_covered` or
`uncovered`. A `non_material` row is no material evaluation and takes no
coverage status; its non-material evidence stays in the inventory above.
Available frame count and coordinator visibility are not coverage.

Reuse a valid result after checking its question, subject/basis, evidence and
invalidators. Record reusable judgments and original owner rulings through the
existing work carrier under TICKETS, separately from interpretation.
Changed material premises invalidate affected claims under the invalidators
of `ACTIVATION.md` section 7; a new role or session alone does not. Required independent assessments still supply their own
judgment. Closure requires satisfied applicable obligations, valid evidence,
required judgments/rulings and no active non-closure condition; review is not
itself the closing event.

## Drift Locks, Invalidation, And Revision

Revalidate before activation and disposition:

- outcome lock: Goal, Product outcome, current MVP or release mandate,
  promotion policy, boundary, and re-entry class;
- basis lock: subject, checkpoint, authority, dependency, configuration,
  topology, evidence, and time;
- Product-role lock: Source Project, Product Definition, Development Product,
  Release Cut, Product, Install, Artifact, and dependency roles;
- authority lock: semantic, evaluation, operation, decision, acceptance, and
  publication owners and grants; and
- coverage-and-return lock: material evaluations, interactions, residuals, and
  declared result routes.

List every method, Product, outcome, subject, checkpoint, candidate,
dependency, composition contract, actor, capability, evidence, authority,
workspace, or topology change that invalidates this basis or its derived
activations, results, conjunctions, and coverage claims, each as a named
instance of a material input change or a changed basis (`ACTIVATION.md`
section 7).

Exact review checkpoints and released Products remain immutable under their
own lifecycle law. Their immutability does not make the Source Project building
a successor immutable. A lawful mutation creates a new checkpoint and
invalidates every affected prior result.
