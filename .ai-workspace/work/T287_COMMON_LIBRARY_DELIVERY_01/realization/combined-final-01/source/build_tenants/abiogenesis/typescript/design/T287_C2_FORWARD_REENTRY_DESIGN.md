selected C2 obligation whose preparation succeeded but whose C2 attempt failed
before actor dispatch. It consumes the original closed nested construction
result, the authentic prepared command task, and current native binding and
observation correspondence. It performs no construction. The failed source Run

[Native event-contract compatibility](T287_NATIVE_EVENT_CONTRACT_COMPATIBILITY_DESIGN.md)
owns exact historical-read/current-append profile ancestry; [interrupted
event-resource recovery](T287_INTERRUPTED_EVENT_RESOURCE_RECOVERY_DESIGN.md)
owns physical interrupted acquisition and the existing witnessed stop. Neither
replaces this forward arm's source, currentness, authority or effect bounds.

- Legacy standalone C2 still requires its exact closed source Run/root result.
- Existing same-Run C1/C2 and D1 preserved-result recovery stay byte/owner exact.
- Existing D2 selection, nonempty repair scope, S/V/R identities and C0 law stay

  service, callback controller or alternative native projector is introduced.
