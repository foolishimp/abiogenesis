1 ms. This is the supported host's defined behavior, not an inferred timing
measurement. [Node 20.20.2 timer source documentation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nodejs/node/v20.20.2/doc/api/timers.md)

Copying the existing raw timer into the selected common mechanism can request

in this review.

**Effect and materiality.** The issue changes the physical-effect schedule and
can force a supported attempt to terminate early. HANDLERS-008 requires the
declared timeout/effect envelope; STDO's admitted-domain and foundation
selection obligations prohibit preserving a known host clamp as correct
realization of a declared wait. The joint contract's observable law is already
correct. The missing part is the explicit host-compatible realization

new Product outcome.

**Minimal lawful re-entry and recommendation.** At the existing E03 HOW,

grace/confirmation and the affected owner lease/absolute/command/native-wakeup
