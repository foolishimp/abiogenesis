## Target Truth

ABG has no builder exemption. One versioned tenant-conformance manifest binds
the exact public capability/contract identities and method/rule/source digests.
The existing compiler/rule path inventories the frozen constitutional, design,
realization, proof, ticket, public-seam, and release-claim surfaces and returns
typed diagnostics/dispositions. The current observer/tuner remains a typed C
program over replay truth; no GF2 or second checker is introduced.

## Required Work

1. Define the conformance-manifest carrier as claims over exact public-contract-
   catalog and capability rows, including catalog identity/version/digest.
2. Define exact self-subject inventory and applicability rules.
3. Define typed diagnostic, disposition, waiver/refusal, and exact-cut result carriers.
4. Map checks onto the existing semantic compiler and rule catalog.
5. Define real-tree, seeded-negative, and exact-cut proof lanes.
6. Define observer/tuner truthful halt, grounded draft, actor/policy
   ratification, replay, and injected-negative proofs on the self-build path.
7. Explicitly exclude universal auto-repair, a new checker, and future GF2 composition.

## Closure Law

Close when T-232 can implement the exact self-conformance and observer/tuner
proof through existing compiler/C/runtime surfaces without a separate method
engine, release harness, or hidden manual interpretation.

## Non-Closure Conditions

- The conformance manifest omits a definition-bearing public capability or contract.
- ABG's own builder surfaces receive exemptions unavailable to downstream tenants.
- Checks are duplicated in a new release-wide checker.
- Observer drafts mutate authority without attributed ratification.
- Review/Consensus/homeostatic GF2 is required for closure.

## Proof Surface

- manifest-to-authority completeness walk
- rule applicability and diagnostic carrier review
- self-subject inventory review
- observer/tuner authority-flow review
- phase-end independent design review against T-218 and PRODUCT
