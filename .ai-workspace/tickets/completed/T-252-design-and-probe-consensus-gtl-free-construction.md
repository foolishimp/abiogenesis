## Intake Triage

1. **Demand**: A calling agentic builder must invoke the published Consensus
   GraphFunction over a ticket or other typed subject and receive attributed
   agreement, dissent, ruling, result, lineage, and replay truth.
2. **Observed defect**: the prior realization placed the constructive body in
   a Consensus-specific TypeScript plugin. It was not a graph and was reverted.
3. **Smallest re-entry**: current product and requirements already require the
   bounded feature. The missing authority is the lawful realization design and
   compiler probe, so this ticket is one `design_reframe`. T-253 preserved that
   topology and closed structural HOF child/member/vector identity, canonical
   `gtl.hof_application` data, and constructor ownership. At intake it did not
   close the unsafe caller-selected native type parameter; T-266 subsequently
   rebound the T-253 API and proofs to constructor-inferred Node/interface
   witnesses. The same bounded re-entry
   exposed the missing generic `(GraphFunction, GraphVector) -> declared C`
   selection relation and routes it to singular prerequisite T-254 without
   changing the accepted Consensus topology. Re-authoring the complete body then
   exposed a second generic relation: `recurse`, `fan_in`, and `gate` did not
   publish complete discriminated `gtl.graph_function_application`
   declarations, leaving source-owned composition unexplained under their
   derived execution subjects. T-265 owns that M01/M02 declaration family and
   M03 `GraphFunctionApplicationLineageProjection`; T-252 may not rehost or
   rewrite those declarations locally.
4. **Probability and proportionality**: the material risk is another category
   error hidden behind working systems code. The proportionate hedge is one
   reviewed domain/sequence/state design, exact structural relation plus native
   type witness, and a no-execution compiler probe. This
   ticket adds no hostile-desktop hardening, scheduler, service framework, or
   feature-specific engine law.
5. **Fresh body-design defects**: the candidate overloaded vector arrays with
   contextual metadata, declared no reachable effect identities, published copied
   operators without a unique name/value registry law, and treated DS-4 SYSTEM
   ownership as though it were DS-1 GTL body truth. It also used direct plugin
   URIs as domain `Operator.binding` values and confused declaration counts with
   observed execution. These are design defects, not implementation permission.
   Existing public `GraphVector.source`, `GraphFunction.effects`,
   `Module.operators`, `abg.hog_handler_bindings`, and `abg.plugin_selection`
   carriers are sufficient to express the six body relations as canonical data;
   they do not supply the missing native Node/interface witness routed to T-266. The
   owner/catalog stamp remains DS-4 admission.

  boundary;

  prove its relation to the explicit reviewer vector;
- `C.retry` is same-contract retry under the declared retryable-failure
  allowlist only. It shall never implement `recurse_next_round`;
- the submitting actor identity never selects the submitter-response worker.
  A separate declared submitter role/worker-selection, instruction-contract,
  result-contract, capability, and configuration binding owns that F_P turn;
- each reviewer profile owns its own role-or-worker-selection contract.
  Ordinary ABG selection resolves it to a worker assignment, and backend and
  transport derive from that assignment. No panel-global agent contract or
  independently selected backend/transport is lawful. The authored GTL
  assignment carries only the selection-contract ref; concrete worker,
  backend, and transport identity remain ABG runtime truth;
- initial semantic assessment and post-submitter semantic reassessment are
  distinct native carriers. Only the post-submitter carrier, joined to the
  exact admitted submitter-response ref, may route to `recurse_next_round`;
- every helper GraphFunction is module-local. This bounded feature does not
  publish the reserved `gtl://abg/review/*` nameplates as a generic Review
  product;
- the canonical outer target is `ConsensusResult`. A round-outcome target is
  an internal relation and cannot satisfy the public GraphFunction contract;
- ABG owns GraphCall/frame/C-call execution, admission, continuation, events,
  replay, and closure; and
- `escalate_fh` is typed stop truth through the ordinary F_H boundary, not a
