## Outcome

Deliver the fixed ABIogenesis 5.0 Product through five installed feature waves.
Conserve working 4.6 behavior, correct only demonstrated 5.0 deltas, reuse one
common implementation for recurring information-technology mechanics, and
expose one installed Product path.

The current selection is narrower than the Wave 2 exit. S1 must establish one
source-blind, non-closing development gate over an exact prebuilt ABIogenesis
artifact: its sole Product-owned root-binding carrier and owner-bound publication verify,
a fresh offline package-manager extraction contains the same complete Product
bytes, and Product can issue the receipt-fed root declaration selection before
runtime. S1 does not execute that selection.

Product and requirements define WHAT. The accepted TypeScript design defines
HOW. This ticket records order, gates, status, and nonclosure; it does not
duplicate the design's entity schemas, equations, foundations, file map,
mutation matrix, or delivery mechanics.
