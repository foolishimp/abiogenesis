## Revision 4 Ruling

Revision 3 preserved the complete intake history but was too holistic to serve
as a delivery plan: 58 detailed requirement candidates, 21 capability
packages, 12 phase handles, a release-wide design pack, and broad negative portfolios
made low-probability defensive work capable of displacing the constructive use
case.

Revision 4 preserves revision 3 in git and applies these rulings:

1. Correctness remains mandatory on supported public paths and every
   closure-bearing truth path.
2. The supported environment is one trusted developer desktop.
3. A sunny-day installed vertical slice precedes broad hardening.
4. Malformed GTL is primarily a native-type, raw-admission, lint, and semantic-
   compiler concern.
5. Malformed, incomplete, or contradictory F_P output is a response-admission
   concern and must become typed non-close truth before writes or replay
   closure.
6. Deterministic configuration and capability failures are preflight failures,
   not retryable F_P failures.
7. Hostile in-process forgery, symlink games, signing, cryptographic
   substitution, and filesystem tamper-proofing are outside 5.0 unless a
   concrete reproduction or a new hostile transport/trust-boundary requirement
   reprices them. Repository and Git transport are trusted for 5.0; digests
   prove coherence, not hostile authenticity.
8. Review findings block a leaf when they are reachable on a supported path,
   corrupt truth, violate accepted authority, or falsify that leaf's closure
   condition. Other findings become separate intake and do not create an
   unbounded reviewer-approval gate.
9. Definition-bearing claims remain non-debt-eligible. Proportionality narrows
   the claim before target freeze; it does not retain a headline and waive its
   proof.
10. Proportionality governs delivery order, defensive depth, and proof reuse.
    It does not remove present-tense product functionality. The autonomous
    path lands first; the complete current operator product and ABG
    self-conformance land in later mandatory phases before release.

`DEC-5.0-PROP-001` is the common decision reference for the revision-4
candidate dispositions below. It is a target-scope decision, not
constitutional promotion, implementation authorization, or T-221 closure.
`DEC-5.0-FULL-001` supersedes only its over-broad deferral of current
interactive-operator and self-conformance truth. It does not re-admit future
GF2, scheduler, hosted-marketplace, multi-host, or hostile-local-tamper scope.

## Accepted 5.0 Target

ABG 5.0 is one source-independent installed GTL/ABG product for a trusted
developer desktop. It installs and binds exact immutable catalog products,
admits their contributions into one ABG-owned catalog, exposes published
GraphFunctions as the sole named callable programs through a public SDK and a
thin `abg.cli`, executes the complete typed C algebra through declared stage
interiors, admits and materializes worker results through declared contracts,
projects result and replay truth, publishes the complete current operator
grammar and interactive start/stop/gaps/F_H/resume loop, applies its
specification-conformance law to its own builder surfaces, builds the next
installed candidate through a two-stage self-hosting gate, and ships as one
exact frozen release.

odd_glc is the first independently released lifecycle-flavoured catalog
product. Its future G5 release proves declarations-only consumption and a
full data-mapper campaign over the frozen ABG 5 candidate. Claude, Codex, and
other host marketplaces remain optional adapters over the same native public
contract. The single 5.0 adapter is a trivial Codex CLI/skill projection over
the public `abg.cli` or SDK contract; it owns no runtime behavior and native
operation requires no host.

Public distribution for 5.0 means an immutable repository/Git release tarball
that a source-blind downstream consumer can install and verify. It does not
require an npm-registry publication, hosted marketplace, signing service, or
storefront.

## Proportional Defense Allocation

The percentages are planning controls, not product truth. A concrete
reproduction overrides the prior.

| Failure class | Directional likelihood | Required 5.0 treatment |
|---|---:|---|
| native GTL carrier or category mismatch | 15-35% | native constructors, discriminated unions, generics, and type tests |
| malformed serialized GTL, duplicate, or invalid value | 10-25% | closed raw admission and exact compiler diagnostics |
| unresolved ref, catalog membership, or program relation | 8-20% | semantic compiler over the authoritative submitted root |
| role/fibre/arm or implementation mismatch | 5-15% | compiled execution-declaration admission |
| missing, truncated, invalid, or wrongly shaped F_P output | 2-10% | closed response/artifact admission before writes and closure |
| contradictory disposition or omitted attestation | 1-5% | one close-eligibility predicate plus necessary corroboration |
| version, binding, allowlist, or agent capability mismatch | 1-5% | one typed preflight; deterministic failures are non-retryable |
| accidental stale local construction basis | 0.1-1% | cheap construction-time coherence only |
| hostile local forgery or filesystem/cryptographic tamper | below 0.1% | deferred unless evidence or distribution promises reprice it |

Defensive work should normally remain within 20-25% of each realization leaf.
A truth-corrupting failure with at least 1% plausible likelihood is mandatory.
A 0.1-1% failure is implemented only when the defense is cheap and does not
reshape the architecture. Below 0.1%, defer unless the harm is irreversible.

Within the defensive budget, prefer approximately:

- 45% native GTL typing, raw admission, and semantic compiler;
- 35% malformed or contradictory F_P response admission;
- 15% version, ref, binding, allowlist, CLI, and capability coherence; and
- 5% exact release reproducibility.

There is no planned 5.0 budget for hostile-local tamper hardening.

## Deferral Law

Delivery order and product scope are separate decisions. A current product
capability may land in a later dependent phase; lateness, design depth, or
implementation cost does not justify deferral.

T-218 may defer a candidate only when all of these hold:

1. it is not present-tense INTENT, PRODUCT, or requirement law for the 5.0
   product;
2. none of the supported release use cases or definition-bearing claims needs
   it;
3. its admission would add a distinct future product, deployment environment,
   trust boundary, lifecycle, or shared-method obligation rather than complete
   the current product; and
4. the register names the evidence or product decision that causes re-entry.

Under this test, the interactive operator loop, complete current operator
grammar, ABG self-conformance, and existing observer/tuner qualification are
5.0 work in later phases. New Review/Consensus/GF2 composition, ABG-owned
scheduling, hosted-store operation, multi-user administration, multiple-host
portfolios, general install lifecycle, and hostile-workstation security remain
future product scope.

## Design And Review Contract

Design depth scales with the changed boundary:

1. Every leaf cites accepted WHAT and names its owning module and public or
   internal boundary.
2. Every leaf declares typed inputs, outputs, errors, owner, and consumers.
3. A new structural or authority boundary receives the required target map,
   IACS, carrier diagram, break order, and mixed-state proof.
4. A local realization that preserves existing design does not create a
   release-wide design pack or new ontology.
5. Native types are taken as far as they can express local validity; the
   semantic compiler owns cross-reference and whole-program relations;
   runtime admission owns environmental facts and F_P results.
6. Each leaf proves the sunny-day supported path, the probable negative
   boundaries it owns, and installed integration where applicable.
7. One bounded independent review follows objective gates. Reachable
   truth-corrupting findings repair the leaf; non-load-bearing findings become
   separate intake with their own probability and use-case assessment.
8. Ticket closure is its stated objective condition, not reviewer approval.

The 5.0 release gate follows this order:

   closed file census;

   timeout, and required capability once;

10. after equivalence passes, freeze `R5 := C1`, pack those exact bytes, inspect
   the closed file census, fresh-install only that tarball, and rerun Hello
   World and the primary interactive loop over exact I1/R5;
11. run the declarations-only G5 data-mapper campaign over exact I1/R5;
12. rerun A5-SH0 over ABG's own frozen builder surfaces and A5-SH4 over the
    self-build observer/tuner path; both must return typed green exact-cut
    results;

    no-direct-orchestration structural differential;

    model and refuse the cut if any mandatory gate is red;

invalidating R5 and rerunning steps 9 through 14.

consume the F_P retry budget. Malformed F_P output after a successful transport
remains a bounded retry/non-close concern.
