## Candidate State Model

Candidate state is independent of ticket `status`:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `proposed` | Captured from intake but not yet reviewed against current authority and realization. |
| `under_review` | Current-tree facts and boundaries are being verified. |
| `needs_evidence` | The claim cannot be dispositioned without additional current proof. |
| `needs_F_H` | Evidence is sufficient; a scope or policy ruling remains. |
| `admitted_to_target` | F_H selected the requirement for the 5.0 target; constitutional promotion is still required. |
| `rejected` | The candidate does not enter the target. |
| `deferred` | The candidate is durable but outside 5.0 with a named trigger. |
| `split` | The candidate was decomposed into named registered successors; it is terminal only after every `split_into` successor is terminal. |
| `superseded` | A named registered `superseded_by` candidate replaces it while preserving history; it is terminal only after that successor is terminal. |

The requirement and package tables also use register-posture labels. These are
not terminal candidate states:

- `current_boundary`: a verified ownership or current-reality correction that
  governs how candidates are classified;
- `retained_baseline`: survived the revision-2 review and remains working input;
- `directed_candidate`: the operator explicitly requested gathering and vetting;
- `supporting_candidate`: may support qualification but is not product truth;
- `expansion_under_review`, `replacement_under_review`,
  `GF2_dependency_under_review`, and `boundary_corrected`: describe how new
  intake changes a retained package.

None is equivalent to `admitted_to_target` or constitutional admission.

Every terminal disposition must record the decision date, rationale, promoting
surface or leaf ticket, and any superseded candidate identity. A prior-release
prerequisite instead records its existing owner and exact closure evidence.

## STDO Intake Triage

### S: Specification Method

T-218 itself remains `goal_reprice`: it is deciding the bounded work wave.
Accepted candidates must then re-enter separately at the first missing layer:

- `product_reprice`: native catalog-host identity, marketplace product shape,
  lawful-program language, `abg.cli` product position, public distribution, and
  the released-GLC relationship;
- `requirement_reprice`: product manifests, install/bind/admit separation,
  catalog queries, public invocation, session allowlists, lawful actions, F_H
  operations, host adapters, Review/Consensus execution, and certification;
- `design_reframe`: marketplace modules, non-graph registry semantics, graph
  shell, external ignition, Review/Consensus composition, adapter projections,
  equivalence, and source/install/materialization separation;
- `realization_refactor`: code and proof only after the relevant requirement and
  design authority is current.

No leaf ticket may carry more than one change class. The flow remains:

`GOALS -> INTENT decision -> PRODUCT -> requirements -> design -> realization -> proof -> release`

Public third-party consumption still needs an explicit decision: it may be a
product reprice under current intent or an intent reprice if it becomes a new
directional commitment.

### T: Ticket Method

The gathering and vetting contract is active and authorized. Its contained
feature candidates are not admitted by that authorization. T-218 closes after
target admission and leaf decomposition, not after
the cross-repo release wave. Every accepted constitutional, design,
realization, sibling-repo, and release concern receives an independently
closeable ticket.

Comments supply evidence. They do not set candidate status. New product truth,
data contracts, runtime law, closure law, provenance law, and repricing work are
not eligible for generic debt or sprint escrow.

### D: Design Module Method

Candidate modules and carriers remain design input until upstream promotion.
Any accepted module must identify requirement trace, source carrier, public
interface, collaborators, IACS, effect edge, lifecycle, positive proof, and
negative proof. Ingress admission, read-only catalog/query, operator mutation,
CLI adaptation, and external-host projection are distinct interfaces and must
not be collapsed into one generic API package.

### O: ODD Method

- published GraphFunctions remain the constructive carrier;
- a GraphFunction may be presented to an operator as a lawful script or skill,
  but no new `Script` or `Skill` ontology is created;
- catalog products contribute declarations and domain meaning;
- ABG alone owns GraphCall opening, worker dispatch, events, replay,
  continuation, admission, projection, and closure;
- `abg.cli` and external skills are adapters, not second controllers;
- no ad hoc imperative script, product-local loader, prompt loop, or host skill
  owns next-step selection, runtime truth, or closure;
- every retained execution boundary passes the 11.5B execution-authority audit;
  no harness, product catalog, CLI, SDK, or host adapter performs work assigned
  to the GraphFunction, declared operator, worker turn, or ABG runtime.

1. freeze source, scenario authority, product manifests, and catalog identities;

7. qualify and publish one exact immutable candidate.
