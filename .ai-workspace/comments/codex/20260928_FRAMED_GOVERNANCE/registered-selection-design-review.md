## Finding

**RS-D01 — P1, design blocker: generic registered choice is coupled to the
complete One Surface lifecycle.**

Locations in the frozen design: 49–82, 175–185 and 219–223.

The proposed plural form requires `synthesizeModel -> evalGap -> evaluateNext`,
one selected workflow, action evaluation and all three refresh calls. The only
target resolver takes that construction composition, and parent closure is
specified as requiring post-evidence refresh. The smallest installed case also
inherits that entire sequence. The design supplies no selection path independent
of this lifecycle and no material reason why a single closed choice needs model
synthesis and gap refresh.

The source confirms this is a substantive dependency, not merely convenient
terminology. `code/src/gtl/contracts.ts:437–464` makes the four-authority
construction composition optional on a Program. Once selected,
`code/src/validator/validation.ts:1471–1556` requires all four authorities, their
initial/refresh loci, eight terms and mandatory post-evidence refresh. Current
intent admission also recognizes the selector only through that composition
(`code/src/abg/traversal_route.ts:1422–1435`). Extending only this form preserves
those dependencies for every use of the proposed generic choice mechanism.

Concrete counterexample: a caller already has the exact frame, observations,
permitted A/B definitions and input; its total declared rule selects A or B;
its parent contract requires the chosen child's admitted result and applicable
assessment/foldback. It has no separate model-synthesis, gap-analysis or
post-evidence replanning obligation. Under this design it cannot express that
small path without adopting the full construction lifecycle or inventing
pass-through model/gap/refresh work solely to satisfy the carrier.

Product 1012–1022 grants the four authorities to the standard system program;
it does not make that pattern mandatory for generic selection. Product 598–599
leaves lifecycle/completion to the declaring Product, and 1065–1072 makes the
library explicitly selected and forbids an unconditional lifecycle. Product
847–857 additionally requires framework work to follow the actual outcome and
governing dependencies. Existing One Surface behavior remains valid where
chosen; its optional status is lost at the new selection boundary.

Impact: the requested smallest generic path is not constructible as designed
without unnecessary semantic work and its context/admission overhead. This is
a design counterexample, not a measured performance regression or explanation
of PC05-11; no runtime harm was observed in this review.

Smallest correction: revise this S1 design so the declared selector/eligible
targets, admitted choice and selected workflow/foldback relation do not require
the four-authority construction lifecycle. Preserve that lifecycle as an
optional consumer pattern with its existing obligations. Show one real S2
path using only its necessary basis preparation, selection, admitted target,
child result and declared parent evaluation/closure; no synthetic model/gap/
refresh calls. Reuse the existing owners and exact identity/input/effect checks.
This correction does not require another Product feature, C term or runtime.

Recommendation: reprice this bounded S1 design before S2 implementation.
Root retains disposition authority.

## Conserved judgments and residuals

- The fixed-callee/input guard is real and should remain:
  `hog/workflow_lifecycle.ts:398–435` derives the intent input and refuses a
  mismatched selected GraphFunction. Branch selection belongs before reaching
  one of the declared fixed workflow terms, not in callee substitution.
- The mutually exclusive plural form, shared pure target relation and narrow
  admission branch avoid a second selector and preserve ordinary continuation
  elsewhere. The proposal adds no eighth C term or new route/event meaning.
  These sound constraints can survive RS-D01's correction; their implementation
  has not been verified.
- Purpose remains on the exact published GraphFunction. The inspected catalog
  entry retains and hashes that definition; no purpose registry is needed.
- The cited installed harness supports frozen artifacts and installed Public
  entry/readback. Its scratch cleanup makes durable evidence retention
  necessary as the design already states. The consensus assertions demonstrate
  a usable single-choice causal example, not proof of the proposed plural or
  minimal generic path. A/B, scope and contract cases are a bounded proof plan.
- S3 honestly leaves the F_P regime/evidence carrier unresolved. The inspected
  admission at `abg/traversal_route.ts:1570–1572` requires the deterministic
  priority label; the design correctly forbids passing F_P under that label.
  Two fresh S2 runs do not establish S4's changed-evidence recursion. Parent
  obligations and PC05-11 remain open at their original scopes.
