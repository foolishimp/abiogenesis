# Applying a transformation calculus to ABG

**Proposal for the active ABG session, 4 October 2026.**

Make each handoff explicit about information meaning, standing, preserved relations and permitted changes. Apply this consistently to remove repeated definitions, evidence copying and reconstruction of established facts. Different representations and implementations can then share one meaning and reusable evidence.

ABG already has the foundation in its [execution and context calculus](/Users/jim/src/apps/abiogenesis/specification/PRODUCT.md:593). This proposal turns that foundation into a worker scaffold across the existing owners.

## Separate information meaning from computation

The information calculus describes information crossing actor, process, storage and public boundaries. Supplied material, candidate answers, admitted judgments, current observations and reusable evidence have different guarantees. These relations are independent of the traversal algorithm.

The computation calculus describes composition, selection, nesting, recursion and continuation under deterministic, probabilistic and human regimes within the selected Product scope. GTL declares; HoG traverses; implementation owners perform effects; ABG admits runtime truth.

The binding is: **each declared computation realizes an information transformation, and its outcome is admitted under that transformation's contract.** HoG's monadic composition binds declared computations; their information contracts retain the admission and evaluation obligations.

As working notation:

```text
Information contract:  τ relates Input, exact basis and Outcome
Computation:           c : Input -> H(Outcome)
Acceptance:            the actual outcome satisfies τ
```

H denotes computation through HoG and existing owner bindings. Outcomes include declared failures, unresolved forms and attributable effects. Transformation does not imply a total pure function or rollback.

[Moggi's distinction between values and computations](https://maxsnew.com/docs/moggi91.pdf) offers a mathematical starting point. The category interpretation of ABG's types, authority and effects remains a hypothesis; this contract scaffold can be used before formalization.

## Constrain each information transformation

Record the canonical source and destination, subject and basis, preserved relations, permitted changes, effect owner and acceptance evidence in the existing design or grant.

Apply these constraints:

- **Preserve identity and lineage.** A projection or parent wrapper retains the actual original producer. A content change creates a successor subject.
- **Change standing only at its owner.** Parsing an answer does not admit a judgment. F11 findings and the sole AF22 qualification verdict retain their separate responsibilities.
- **Conserve failure and uncertainty.** Missing assessment remains unresolved; it cannot erase an available failure or its citations.
- **Declare information loss.** A projection may omit detail only when its consumer still receives the meaning and evidence required for that responsibility. References must resolve within the consumer's admitted access.
- **Reuse within validity.** Preserve established facts while their dependencies and applicability remain valid. A function or role boundary alone does not require reconstructing their history.
- **Check the join.** The consumer must actually use the producer's meaning, coordinates and evidence. Compatible field shapes alone do not establish this.

This applies existing [context and access](/Users/jim/src/apps/abiogenesis/specification/PRODUCT.md:701) and [valid reuse](/Users/jim/src/apps/abiogenesis/specification/PRODUCT.md:799) law.

## Remove repeated meanings and work

Use one canonical definition per logical contract. Imports, schemas, assets, catalog entries, SDK calls and CLI views retain their declared relation to it. Distinct information states may need distinct contracts; publication and transport views derive their meaning.

The [accepted library target](/Users/jim/src/apps/abiogenesis/build_tenants/abiogenesis/typescript/design/T287_F11_REUSABLE_QUALIFICATION_LIBRARY_TARGET_DESIGN.md:20) already selects this contraction.

| Recurring complexity | Application of the calculus |
|---|---|
| Callers reconstruct framework contracts | Import canonical definitions and name the actual source and destination transformation. |
| Public views agree with each other but omit required content | Map every required identity to its canonical owner and projection; compare publication with the complete requirement population. |
| Immutable evidence travels inside each work value | Carry typed selectors into exact accessible owner resources, retaining required admission and digest binding. |
| Internal handoffs repeat full validation and replay | Consume established facts under their validity relation; perform the checks required by the changed boundary or dependency. |
| Helpers for individual scenarios repeat framework meaning | Parameterize the existing owning transformation with domain material and criteria. |
| Independent component passes leave integration unproved | Exercise the actual installed producer and consumer join with fresh readback. |

Keep preparation, assessment, authentication, aggregation and qualification distinct while reducing duplicated meaning and work.

## Use F11 as the worked case

The relevant information path is:

```text
Exact subject and law
  -> request bound to its responsibility
  -> raw answer and transport observations
  -> canonical judgment construction and native authentication
  -> judgment admitted by ABG
  -> F11 evaluation and admitted result
  -> sole AF22 verdict
  -> release and fresh consumer reads
```

The [F11 carrier diagnosis](/Users/jim/src/apps/abiogenesis/.ai-workspace/comments/codex/20260928_FRAMED_GOVERNANCE/20261002_F11_CARRIER_ROOT_CAUSE_AND_FIX.md:11) documents a frozen input of about 67.8 MB, while its rendered assessor prompt was about 1.02 MB. Repeated partitions and embedded proof bodies expanded the carrier before assessment.

A proposed representation repair retains the inventory and shared partitions through their existing immutable owner, selects each rule's complete effective domain, and supplies exact references to declaration and provenance evidence. The consumer resolves required material and retains its qualifications. Representation, resolution and validation must change together; full coverage and omission checks remain binding.

**Prediction:** for fixed selected material and effective domains, normalization reduces carried bytes, repeated encoding and preparation memory while preserving applicable coverage, original producers, unresolved obligations and permitted dispositions. Adding unrelated evidence bodies should not increase ordinary handoff work without a declared dependency. The [Product cost law](/Users/jim/src/apps/abiogenesis/specification/PRODUCT.md:838) provides the governing relation.

Compare the installed paths, including judgment retention and cold reads. Measure bytes, supplied material, elapsed work and peak RSS by phase. Hold observations and admitted evaluator outputs fixed for conservation checks; fresh stochastic answers need not be identical.

The repair fails if omitted members pass, references cannot be resolved on cold reads, producer identity changes or unrelated bodies still drive handoff cost. Prompt size alone establishes neither reduction nor semantic sufficiency.

The G1 mixed case supplies another discriminator: an authenticated failure for A and missing assessment for B must produce both the failure with its citations and the unresolved obligation. That relation must survive aggregation, AF22 consumption and fresh reads.

## Give workers a compact contract

Use this description in the existing grant or design record; it introduces no runtime carrier.

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Consumes | Canonical input contract, exact preimage, subject, law and required resources. |
| Produces | Canonical output contract and the standing the output may acquire. |
| Preserves | Required identity, producer lineage, coverage, failures, citations and uncertainty. |
| May change | Selected meaning or representation delta and any justified omission. |
| May affect | Declared effect owner and permitted territory. |
| Valid while | Dependencies, applicability and invalidation conditions. |
| Accepted when | Observable positive, nearest negative and affected installed join. |
| Cost evidence | Relevant bytes and work before and after where the change claims a reduction. |

A new interface needs a distinct meaning, authority or effect boundary. Otherwise, import or project the canonical contract. A check needs a named obligation and owner; repetition needs a material reason.
