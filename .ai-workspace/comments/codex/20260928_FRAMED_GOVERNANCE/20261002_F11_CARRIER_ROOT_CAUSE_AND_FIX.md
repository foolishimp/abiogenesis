## Proposed fix in dependency order

1. **Normalize scope without weakening completeness.** Retain inventory/scope once through the existing immutable owner resource. Use shared default or interned partitions with exact per-rule overrides/selectors. Resolve the complete effective partition before validating member/root coverage, disjoint membership, roles, owners, sources, catalog identity and every rule group. Preserve distinct per-rule splits and unknown applicability/grouping. Current contracts have no sparse override fallback: change representation, resolver and validators together under the owner, producing a new candidate. Do not delete [complete-domain checks](/Users/jim/src/apps/abiogenesis/build_tenants/abiogenesis/typescript/code/src/validator/qualification.ts:333). Omitting B from domain `(A,B)` could otherwise escape [downstream checks over declared groups](/Users/jim/src/apps/abiogenesis/build_tenants/abiogenesis/typescript/code/src/validator/self_conformance.ts:227). Product 459 permits shared execution evidence, not omitted members.

2. **Separate proof resources from semantic values.** Give declarations, catalog/readiness, lock, material and provenance typed selectors into exact accessible owner resources. Reuse the R10/C2 establishment and admitted-consumption pattern, not a generic cache, digest trust or new truth store. The initial install/lock receipt lacks native declaration source text and cannot replace that proof alone. Loose paths and hashes establish neither durable access nor authorship. Bind source/producer event, contract, scope, law, digest, authorship, spans and admission as required by the selected relation. Authenticate raw/copied ingress and changed or physical boundaries. Cover successful judgment/result carriers so output embedding does not undo the input repair.

3. **Reuse established immutable facts inside their valid basis.** Avoid repeated full encode/decode, clone and hash work merely at internal handoffs. Preserve each owner's responsibility, invalidation, fresh raw admission, physical checks and cold reconstruction. Make harness error serialization lazy; retain coordinates and useful summaries instead of whole prepared carriers. Harness cleanup alone does not fix the actual CLI path. Heap increases or prompt trimming do not establish closure.

4. **Measure the complete installed path.** Record input, transport, event and materialized bytes; per-phase validation/render/preparation elapsed time, call counts, heap/external memory/RSS; and cold reads. Separate one-time acquisition from ordinary handoff costs. Vary unrelated rule populations, old proof bodies and inputs across invocations to detect renewed copying. Use the full 2,137-rule / 1,950-member fixture plus nearest negatives. Declare a fixture-specific operational envelope from the repaired baseline; do not invent universal quotas or unmeasured numeric targets.
