# STRATEGY: STDO, Representation, and ABIogenesis Product separation

- **Status**: Open
- **Authority**: Proposal commentary
- **Basis**: ABIogenesis-selected STDO `v2.5.0-rc.4`; exact installed Representation and Axiom Indexer RC4; current ABIogenesis Product direction
- **Writing grant**: Direct user request; one Writer activation under the [Worker frame](stdo://releases/v2.5.0-rc.4/standards/STDO_REFERENCE_FRAME_BASELINE.md#worker), with this post as its exact mutation subject and complete write territory; return the source-linked proposal after document checks

The proposed separation assigns STDO the governing meaning, STDO Representation
the means for practitioners and agents to apply it, and ABIogenesis the declared
execution mechanisms around agent participation. The decisive transition is who
holds the control loop. STDO remains constitutional authority in both operating
modes; execution changes from agent coordination to an admitted Program traversed
by HoG with ABG-owned runtime truth and continuation.

This post allocates the complete earlier breakdown: seven model/method revisions,
three delivery revisions, six agent-support enhancements, nine forced-ranked
recommendations, and all thirteen use scenarios. The [original strategy and use
model](20260905T032303Z_STRATEGY_postmortem_reference_frames_roles_method_tuning.md)
retains the scenario definitions, completion conditions, alternatives, cost
model, and incident analysis. The matrices here map that content to proposed
owners and realization responsibilities. They do not create another scenario
catalog. On adoption, both posts should point to the accepted owning surfaces.

The evidence starts with the [delivery-cycle postmortem](20260904T014553Z_HANDOFF_t287_canonical_root_stage1_to_stage2.md#postmortem-findings-from-the-24-hour-delivery-cycle)
and the separate [RC4 updater incident](20260905T030521Z_REPAIR_rc4_context_update_result.md).
The user's control-loop framing supplies the proposed Product separation. The
recommendation remains a proposal for owner intake; it grants no implementation
or change to current constitutional authority.

The three principal responsibilities are:

| Product | What it provides | What drives the work | What its assurance must establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| **STDO** | Normative meaning, authority, constraints, applicability, evidence requirements, and completion conditions; bounded subordinate distribution tooling and workflow skills. | A practitioner interprets and applies the method in a selected consumer context. STDO law does not select a universal executor. | The governing relations are coherent, usable, and faithfully projected; shared rules have one owner. |
| **STDO Representation** | Source-linked compression, navigable constraints, native skills, bounded context, examples, and use of selected mechanical tools. | In native agent use, the LLM interprets, selects frames, coordinates work, consumes results, and continues within its grant. | Agents can achieve the selected STDO use outcomes through supported entry paths, preserve authority, recover correctly, and meet declared cost bounds. |
| **ABIogenesis, including GTL, HoG, and ABG** | Declared executable composition, bounded deterministic/agent/human work, exact effect and admission boundaries, admitted outcomes, replay, and durable continuation. | The admitted Program and runtime govern progression. Agent construction and judgment occur through declared participation. | The exact installed causal path enforces declared boundaries and reconstructs admitted execution facts, including interruption, refusal, correction, and continuation. |

The middle responsibility already exists. The installed [Representation Product](../../../.genesis/development-products/stdo-representation/specification/PRODUCT.md#product-statement)
includes native instructions and explicitly retains the LLM as semantic harness.
Skills are already Product members. Its current boundary excludes a local semantic
compiler, GTL engine, prompt orchestrator, and model runtime.

The separation preserves existing supporting owners. STDO already owns its
bounded manager and shared workflow plugin. [Axiom Indexer](../../../.genesis/development-products/axiom-indexer/specification/PRODUCT.md)
remains a distinct dependency Product for resolution, validation, indexing, and
pure joining. Grouping these capabilities into an operating mode does not move
their ownership or collapse their releases. The STDO boundary was checked against
source commit `7a25668a8fecfd26f895759af3bec4708727964a`, path
`specification_methodology/specification/PRODUCT.md`; its [source-project Product
surface](../../../../specification_methodology/specification_methodology/specification/PRODUCT.md)
is the authoring destination for a selected successor change.

The ABG inversion is: **the agent becomes a participant invoked within the
declared process; the admitted process holds continuation.** GTL declares the
work and contracts, HoG traverses the admitted GTL value, and ABG admits execution
facts and owns runtime continuation. An agent's response can supply a candidate
or required judgment. The response alone cannot establish authoritative runtime
completion. This follows the [ABIogenesis Product statement](../../../specification/PRODUCT.md#product-statement)
and its exact owner boundaries; it is a target responsibility, not proof that
the complete STDO-use realization has been delivered.

One allocation needs to be explicit to make this implementable: **who publishes
the STDO-specific executable Program?** The recommendation is that Representation
own a future, separately selected STDO-use realization profile containing the
accepted STDO model's explicit GTL encoding and domain GraphFunctions.
ABIogenesis supplies the generic language, validation, traversal, runtime, and
admission capabilities that profile uses. This is a proposed extension of
Representation's current Product scope, requiring its own Product re-entry.
It adds no Representation executor: HoG still directly traverses the declared
Program and ABG still owns runtime truth.

This gives Representation two possible realization profiles over the same user
outcomes: native agent use, and use through an explicitly selected ABIogenesis
Program. Native use remains useful independently. An ABIogenesis run can reuse
Representation's source routes and bounded context for agent calls without
letting those instructions take over run progression. A profile switch binds
the chosen work, basis, outstanding obligations, and continuation owner
explicitly. A blocked runtime does not implicitly hand its authority to chat.

The current `a_c.STDO` compression is a navigation model with stated residuals.
It is not already a complete accepted executable STDO model. Any GTL realization
needs an explicit accepted model-to-carrier relation, preserved meanings,
declared judgments, refusal conditions, and evidence. Index validity or a shared
name cannot establish that relation. The [Axiomatic Calculus](stdo://releases/v2.5.0-rc.4/standards/AXIOMATIC_CALCULUS.md)
and each declaring Product retain their own meanings and authority.

The ten revisions below retain the earlier breakdown and specify where the work
lands. Existing sufficient law should be applied or projected correctly; a new
rule is justified only by an identified insufficiency.

1. **R01 — Adopt the logical Product lifecycle and use model.** STDO's owning
   Product/scenario surface owns the common user outcomes and completion
   conditions. It covers understanding applicability, establishing context,
   using and evolving it, and suspending or concluding participation. Individual
   work proceeds through outcome, proportionate treatment, authorized action,
   condition evaluation, and conclusion or continuation. Observation, decisions,
   interruption, and recovery can occur wherever relevant. These remain logical
   functions rather than prescribed screens, schemas, or agent stages.

   Representation requirements bind supported entry, guidance, context, and
   continuity behavior to that model. Its proposed executable profile binds the
   same outcomes to declared STDO-use Programs. ABIogenesis requirements own the
   generic capabilities those Programs need. Acceptance is evidence from ordinary
   supported use, beginning with first useful action, rather than possession of
   a scenario document. All thirteen scenarios participate.

2. **R02 — Make proportionate classification a supported capability.** STDO owns
   the lawful treatment and re-entry relations in [Spec Method](stdo://releases/v2.5.0-rc.4/standards/SPEC_METHOD.md),
   [Reference Frame Method](stdo://releases/v2.5.0-rc.4/standards/REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md),
   and [Ticket Method](stdo://releases/v2.5.0-rc.4/standards/TICKET_METHOD.md).
   Ordinary requests require judgment between local realization, a design
   boundary, a Product/requirement boundary, or operational handling, with
   information-only and uncertain cases also supported. These descriptions route
   into existing re-entry law; they do not replace its distinctions.

   Representation supplies material facts, examples, source routes, and the
   cheapest useful discriminator. The agent makes and can revise the judgment.
   The future STDO-use Program declares the applicable branches and bounded
   judgment call; ABG admits only progression supported by the declared grant
   and required results. Qualification begins with an unclassified request:
   sufficient local work adds no unnecessary review, a material Public change
   is detected before promotion, and a changed governing relation reaches its
   owner. Wrong classification must be corrected within a declared allowance.
   USE-02 is the headline capability.

3. **R03 — Express closure as a condition.** STDO owns the requirement that the
   declared completion obligations hold on the exact subject, with valid
   applicable evidence and no active non-closure condition. Required semantic
   judgments, independent assessment, and reserved owner decisions remain
   explicit inputs where the governing boundary calls for them. Holding a review
   is neither a sufficient nor a universal closing condition.

   Representation exposes the condition, evidence, and missing inputs, and
   guides authorized updates to the existing owning record. Its executable
   profile declares the corresponding condition and result dependencies.
   ABIogenesis realizes evaluation, admission, and replay under those contracts.
   Runtime completion and completion of a consumer ticket remain distinct claims
   unless an explicit owner-defined relation joins them. USE-03 and USE-05 must
   reject false closure and avoid an extra review merely to produce an event.

4. **R04 — Separate computed facts, judgment, owner rulings, frames, and actors.**
   STDO owns these semantic and authority distinctions through the Axiomatic
   Calculus, Reference Frame Method, and reference-frame baseline. The use model's
   **C** means computed facts and declared deterministic checks; **J** means
   contextual judgment; **O** means a reserved owner ruling. These are explanatory
   annotations, not new functor kinds. Concrete ABIogenesis bindings must preserve
   their declared `F_D`, `F_P`, and `F_H` meanings without automatic cross-context
   equivalence.

   Representation gets deterministic facts from tools and asks an agent or owner
   only for the material unresolved judgment. Frame selection establishes the
   questions and obligations; actor allocation follows independence, capability,
   attention, and work needs. The executable profile declares those dependencies
   and participation contracts. ABIogenesis enforces the admitted contracts and
   continuation. It does not turn semantic judgment into determinism or require
   one agent per frame. USE-02, 05, 06, 07, 08, and 11 are the principal cases.

5. **R05 — Make owner decisions durable and status a truthful projection.** STDO
   owns decision authority, the distinction between a ruling and its
   interpretation, and the existing ownership of work/status surfaces. The owner
   should receive the question, exact subject, current condition, material
   uncertainty, options, consequences, and cost in a short decision account.
   Each distinct ruling is recorded once in its owning carrier; consuming agents
   recover that ruling and only its authorized consequences.

   Representation owns preparing and navigating that account and consuming
   source-bound projections. In executable use, the STDO Program identifies
   required human input and its domain consequences; ABIogenesis supplies the
   declared human boundary, admitted response, and continuation. Consumers own
   ticket and decision semantics; ABG owns runtime events. A conversational GO
   cannot override a durable HOLD, and a stale view cannot author either state.
   USE-11, 06, and the status parts of USE-02/03 prove continuity without repeated
   owner authorization.

6. **R06 — Reprice documentation obligations and clarify evidence reuse.** The
   owning changes belong in the STDO baseline and [Design Module Method](stdo://releases/v2.5.0-rc.4/standards/DESIGN_MODULE_METHOD.md),
   with their conditions reflected in projections. Three specific issues require
   examination: the baseline overview describes independent review
   unconditionally while its 5A branch is conditional; the three-view gate
   requires all views at every new/materially changed typed or semantic boundary;
   and closure reconstruction needs clearer conditions for reusing accepted
   relations after a bounded repair.

   The proposal colocates each obligation with its applicability trigger,
   proportionality limit, and invalidation/reuse condition. Required design
   representations should resolve material uncertainty; a stateful effect
   boundary can justify views that a stateless boundary does not need.
   Representation projects the resulting rules faithfully. An executable
   STDO-use profile encodes their conditional applicability rather than a fixed
   document/review sequence. ABIogenesis enforces that declared composition.
   Current RC4 and stronger accepted consumer conditions continue to govern until
   changed. The cycle establishes churn; it does not measure the three-view
   rule's separate cost. USE-02, 04, and 05 provide the qualifying cases.

7. **R07 — Make usability and cost part of Product qualification.** STDO owns the
   logical user/cost obligations and evidence distinctions. Representation owns
   qualification of native use and its proposed STDO-use realization profile.
   ABIogenesis owns qualification of the general installed execution path and
   runtime accounting capabilities. The consumer owns the relevant claim and
   accepted case limits.

   Cases declare expected/maximum activations, elapsed time, method overhead,
   available input/cache/output usage, owner attention, and correction/recovery
   allowances before qualification. Functional validity and cost performance are
   reported separately: a valid result that exceeds its envelope fails the
   efficiency claim. Missing usage remains unknown. Cost controls preserve all
   required conditions. Orientation, first useful action, return after
   interruption, and understandable conclusion are Product outcomes. This applies
   to all selected scenarios and material combinations.

8. **R08 — Improve existing native entry, skills, and compact context.** Shared
   `stdo help`, `ticket`, `work`, `review`, and `status` workflows remain with
   STDO's plugin. Compression/map navigation, selected frame details, and
   caller-authored bounded requests remain with Representation's native skill.
   [T-024](../../../../specification_methodology/specification_methodology/.ai-workspace/tickets/completed/T-024-make-stdo-downstream-use-operational.md)
   already completed shared downstream-use work; [T-025](../../../../specification_methodology/specification_methodology/.ai-workspace/tickets/completed/T-025-bind-proportional-work-carrier-selection.md)
   already completed proportional carrier selection. Revisions should repair
   measured gaps in these capabilities and keep their responsibilities coherent.

   Ordinary requests should reach the appropriate path; existing authority and
   known answers should be reused. Read-only use retains its effect boundary.
   A bounded activation includes the outcome, exact basis and subject, grant,
   material sources, current evidence, exclusions, and return/stop conditions.
   Detail is reacquired when material. Examples teach classification and recovery
   without becoming extra law. In runtime use this guidance serves bounded agent
   calls while the admitted Program retains progression. USE-12, 01, 02, 05, 06,
   and 11 measure practical benefit rather than prompt brevity alone.

9. **R09 — Move computable work to its declared tool owner.** Computed resolution,
   preimage capture, explicit dependency comparisons, evidence binding/freshness,
   status projection, diagnostic preservation, and accounting need reproducible
   outputs. STDO defines the relevant conditions; Representation makes those
   outputs usable and exposes what remains judged. It must not ask agents to
   repeatedly narrate facts an available tool can recover.

   Existing ownership determines the implementation: distribution checks go to
   STDO's manager, axiomatic mechanics to Axiom Indexer, worksite observations and
   effects to the selected worksite tools, qualification joins to the owning
   proof tooling, and runtime admission/replay to ABIogenesis. A new generic
   read-only context assembly capability is a proposed Representation scope
   change over owner-supplied facts, not permission to add a ticket executor to
   STDO or selection logic to Axiom Indexer's pure joiner. Missing capabilities
   require explicit Product allocation before implementation. USE-01, 05, 06,
   08, and 09 are the principal cases; the detailed allocation follows below.

10. **R10 — Complete the explicitly selected consumer update.** The shared
    successor contract belongs to STDO's toolchain owner through existing backlog
    [T-029](../../../../specification_methodology/specification_methodology/.ai-workspace/tickets/backlog/T-029-complete-consumer-cohort-adoption.md).
    RC4 `adopt` changes only the selected STDO basis and basis-relative schema
    locator. That result cannot establish complete companion adoption.

    The proposed outcome resolves and verifies the explicit cohort, exact
    companion releases, authorized consumer bindings, native routes, and the
    freshness of dependent context. Representation/Axiom Indexer own their
    release identities and projection contracts; consumers own their explicit
    selection. Semantic re-authoring remains a declared judgment task when a
    derived map cannot be refreshed mechanically. ABIogenesis may eventually
    execute an admitted adoption Program, but runtime execution does not move
    updater semantics into ABI Product law. Partial completion must remain
    visible and prior usable state survive failure. USE-09, supported by 01 and
    06, proves the complete ordinary update. T-029 is recorded, not implemented
    or admitted by this proposal.

This lifecycle is also the UX model. The practitioner should be able to understand
what can be done, reach a first useful outcome, see the current condition, supply
a necessary decision, recover progress, and conclude use. Further detail is
available when needed. Neither realization should make users navigate internal
frame names, records, runtime mechanics, or the complete method before those
details help them act. UX design follows these functional relationships; this
proposal selects no screen layout or UI framework.

The role and reference-frame changes have a separate allocation because a runtime
does not remove the need for contextual responsibility.

ABI's current [agent instructions](../../../AGENTS.md#map-first-reference-frames)

One truth surface means one authored authority for each relation, with explicit
references between owners. The complete selected STDO cut owns method law;
consumer Product authority owns its domain outcome; the accepted STDO-use model
binds the application's interpretation to exact source authority; its admitted GTL Program owns executable
composition; ABG owns runtime facts. Decision carriers own their exact rulings,
and current work carriers retain their assigned state. Maps, prompts, status
accounts, and dashboards derive from those sources. A second manually maintained
copy is not a continuity mechanism.

Qualification must distinguish the two assurance mechanisms. Native use proves
that real supported agent entry, source acquisition, tool use, judgment,
construction, owner interaction, and continuation achieve the selected outcome.
Executable use additionally proves the installed Program-to-owner-to-admission
path and fresh replay. A model returning the expected words, skill discovery,
correct metadata, or a validator pass cannot alone establish either complete
use claim. UAT proves the supported user outcome; E2E proves the exact installed
causal path; integration/combinatorial evidence covers material composition;
unit evidence remains bounded to its module-owned properties. The [STDO testing
frames](stdo://releases/v2.5.0-rc.4/standards/STDO_REFERENCE_FRAME_BASELINE.md#derived-user-acceptance-frame)
retain those distinct responsibilities.

Each case declares its condition oracle, alternatives, and cost envelope
independently before the candidate acts. The agent receives ordinary inputs and
available evidence, not the expected classification. Comparable native and
executable cases retain the same functional conditions while recording host,
subject, required independence, external waits, and mechanism differences.
Runtime execution is a change in control and assurance; whether it costs less
must be measured.

The structural cost targets from the original model remain: zero extra
independent review rounds when no governing condition requires one; zero repeated
owner rulings for an unchanged resolved question; zero manual reconstruction of
facts recoverable as valid computed outputs; and zero repeated satisfied
obligations without material invalidation. Numeric activation, time, token, and
owner-attention limits remain case-specific calibration inputs. Until selected,
their bounded-cost claims cannot be qualified. Retain total cost across all
actors as well as method overhead so moving work between labels cannot simulate
an improvement. A reached limit causes the declared stop or repricing, preserving
safe effect handling and required evidence.

The implementation sequence should use the mature native operating mode to
resolve uncertainty while ABIogenesis reaches the relevant runtime capabilities.

Native improvements proceed under their sufficient current authority.
Executable-profile ownership and scope admission gate the executable work, not
independently authorized native improvements.

1. **Select the common model.** Resolve the logical lifecycle, thirteen scenario
   conditions, C/J/O boundaries, and cost calibration. Resolve the nonclaim/debt
   question separately where a selected case depends on it.
   These are bounded owner decisions, not one approval ceremony per table row.
2. **Establish ordinary-use baseline evidence.** Prioritize USE-02's minimum
   sufficient path and cheap correction, then owner continuity and resumption.
   Include first-use readiness and conclusion so the model covers the full user
   relationship. Classify each observed gap as inadequate law, incorrect
   projection, missing computation, unsupported interaction, or failure to follow
   sufficient law. This prevents implementing a mechanism to compensate for the
   wrong defect.
3. **Make selected shared and native corrections at their owners.** Resolve the
   specific method wording issues; derive the affected skills/compressions; add
   only selected tool capabilities. Use T-029 for complete consumer adoption.
   Qualify supported native use and cost, release the complete relevant cuts,
   and explicitly adopt them. This work can provide value before the executable
   profile exists.
4. **Declare one coherent executable STDO-use path.** Select the proposed
   Representation home for STDO-specific executable artifacts and admit the
   profile's scope/model before executable work. Then express a selected outcome,
   classification, bounded construction, evidence condition, and continuation in
   GTL, using only needed judgment/owner boundaries. Bind exact ABIogenesis capabilities and dependency
   identities. Assess against ABIogenesis's existing Product before proposing a
   new runtime requirement. A consumer use case does not automatically expand
   ABI Product law or select an unrelated runtime redesign.
5. **Qualify, then expand from evidence.** Prove the selected installed path and
   meaningful refusal/interruption/correction cases. Extend to other scenarios
   and material combinations as their requirements become supported. Compare
   accepted outcomes, costs, reopened claims, discarded evidence, and escaped
   defects. Preserve qualified native operation while the runtime profile
   matures; support any transition between them through an explicit basis and
   continuation relation.

Release and consumer adoption remain separate conditions throughout. The shared
method route is [intake, owning authority, consistency review, acceptance,
complete release, and explicit adoption](stdo://releases/v2.5.0-rc.4/standards/POSTING_GUIDE.md#shared-method-adoption-and-propagation-stdo-up-012).
Additional carriers, diagrams, agents, or review rounds arise only from an
applicable requirement. A proposal table is not an activation manifest.
