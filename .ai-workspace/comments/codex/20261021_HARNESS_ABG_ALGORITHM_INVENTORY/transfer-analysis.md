# Candidate algorithm loans from Harness² into ABG 5.0

Derived comparison commentary, 21 October 2026. These eight groups are bounded candidates selected for analysis by the Executive, **not implementation priority, accepted design, work grants or proof of improvement**. See [the overview](README.md), [Harness's 19 groups](harness-algorithms.md), [ABG's 23 groups](abg-algorithms.md) and [source pins](SOURCES.json).

A loan begins with computational similarity. It then needs semantic **and operational** equivalence evaluation, preserving identity, ownership, ordering, mutation, lifecycle, failure, currentness and authority. Its **semantic disposition** (existing accepted relation, extension or new candidate relation) is separate from its **realisation disposition** (consume, local implementation, optimise, adapt or justified duplication). This applies the selected method's DESIGN_MODULE:1590–1682; it creates no algorithm registry or new universal gate.

Propose–Probe–Compose is already expressible through ABG composition/zoom. The analysis concerns particular policies and mechanisms, their usable execution bindings and supporting evidence. It does not claim new expressibility from the name, Python implementation or lack of a stock named ABG function.

## L01 — Bounded candidate search over existing zoom

**Sources/counterparts:** [H04](harness-algorithms.md#h04), [H05](harness-algorithms.md#h05), [H06](harness-algorithms.md#h06), [H07](harness-algorithms.md#h07) → [A05](abg-algorithms.md#a05), [A06](abg-algorithms.md#a06), [A09](abg-algorithms.md#a09), [A10](abg-algorithms.md#a10), [A12](abg-algorithms.md#a12), [A13](abg-algorithms.md#a13).

**Precise mechanism.** Declare width/depth, invariant/domain proposal lenses, same-task probes, comparative composition, retained alternatives and bounded validation-feedback repair as one explicit policy.

**Semantic disposition.** Existing accepted composition/zoom/call relations supply expressibility and execution ownership. The particular stock search policy is a candidate policy extension; it does not create another semantic runtime, work selector or concept of recursion.

**Realisation disposition.** Adapt or implement the policy locally over existing graph/leaf seams. Consume existing traversal, materialization and child-return relations. A Python service, new tenant or second controller is not a prerequisite.

**Preserved identities, effects and authority.** Original outcome, declared GraphFunction/Program identities, exact input and permitted choice domain, operation grants, effect owners, admitted Result/J provenance and parent closure. Refining a task contract does not authorize runtime GTL publication.

**Plausible benefit, unmeasured.** Potentially better candidate diversity and evidence-responsive refinement; both quality and resource benefit are unmeasured.

**Next decisive test.** Run existing ABG policy and the declared width/depth policy on the same frozen workflow/tasks, with matched actual model/permission/resource budget and independent original acceptance criteria. Retain every failed probe and compare outcome quality as well as total work.

**Limits.** Latest sequential composition can regress; parallel diversity does not imply semantic diversity or monotonic improvement. Expressibility is established by current composition/zoom support, while this particular policy and its installed efficacy are separate claims.

## L02 — Role evidence packs and anonymous comparison

**Sources/counterparts:** [H02](harness-algorithms.md#h02), [H08](harness-algorithms.md#h08) → [A07](abg-algorithms.md#a07), [A08](abg-algorithms.md#a08), [A11](abg-algorithms.md#a11), [A22](abg-algorithms.md#a22).

**Precise mechanism.** Present original source, actual supplied bodies, candidate artifacts, behavior traces, harness differences and decisions as a browseable role-specific pack; optionally anonymise comparative display while retaining the exact reverse map.

**Semantic disposition.** Existing current-context, instruction-projection and independent-assessment relations remain the semantic owners. A particular comparative presentation policy is an extension candidate; exact sharing and approximate compression require different preservation/adequacy claims.

**Realisation disposition.** Adapt the projection/index/diff/anonymous-display mechanisms below existing context/assessment owners; implement locally or consume an equivalent pure renderer where its contract matches.

**Preserved identities, effects and authority.** Source versus observation versus candidate versus admitted fact; exact currentness/dependencies; assessor authorship independence; original criteria; actual prompt/schema and supplied body identities. Anonymous display is not removal of producer identity or admission provenance.

**Plausible benefit, unmeasured.** Potentially more usable evidence and smaller repeated context, unmeasured. File availability alone does not prove the actor read or understood the material.

**Next decisive test.** Compare task/assessor context variants using the same original outcome oracle and exact source. Check every required field/body, source access, reverse identity link and unresolved criterion; measure supplied bytes/tokens and independent judgment agreement. Explicitly test any lossy policy's declared adequacy relation.

**Limits.** Harness projections truncate individual observations and some profiles expose names-only artifacts. Exact deduplication cannot justify silently dropping meaning; anonymity cannot guarantee unbiased judging or independence.

## L03 — Earlier-task experience as candidate context

**Sources/counterparts:** [H11](harness-algorithms.md#h11), [H12](harness-algorithms.md#h12), [H13](harness-algorithms.md#h13) → [A07](abg-algorithms.md#a07), [A09](abg-algorithms.md#a09), [A20](abg-algorithms.md#a20).

**Precise mechanism.** Retain a full adopted artifact with a cue, scoped lesson, evidence and optional confidence; retrieve only from earlier tasks in the declared domain/stream and present it as candidate context.

**Semantic disposition.** Existing reuse/current-applicability law stays authoritative. The concrete experience-selection/reflection policy is a candidate extension requiring F_P relevance/causality judgment and evidence; storing a lesson does not establish admitted applicability.

**Realisation disposition.** Adapt the library/index/retrieval policy under existing context and reuse seams, using current artifact/source owners. Do not introduce a rival truth store or autonomous runtime publication mechanism.

**Preserved identities, effects and authority.** Original producer identity and dependencies, declared domain/task ordering, source access/currentness, model/task separation, assessment status and explicit unresolved applicability. Historical lesson and current instruction retain different standing.

**Plausible benefit, unmeasured.** Potential transfer across related tasks and reduced repeated exploration, unmeasured. Full retained artifacts may improve inspection while increasing storage/context work.

**Next decisive test.** Use held-out later tasks; audit future-task/answer leakage, stale dependencies and retrieval selection. Include no-library and irrelevant-library controls; independently assess lesson support, causal generalisation, actual retrieval usefulness and all-in cost.

**Limits.** Reflection may generalise incorrectly from confounded/noisy probes. Earlier-only is an experiment-order property, not proof of semantic relevance. This is no claim that ABG 5.1's dedicated tuner exists or is selected.

## L04 — Exact reuse fingerprints and invalidation checks

**Sources/counterparts:** [H14](harness-algorithms.md#h14), [H16](harness-algorithms.md#h16) → [A06](abg-algorithms.md#a06), [A08](abg-algorithms.md#a08), [A17](abg-algorithms.md#a17), [A19](abg-algorithms.md#a19), [A20](abg-algorithms.md#a20), [A21](abg-algorithms.md#a21).

**Precise mechanism.** Bind pure-work/experiment reuse to exact input/context/artifact fingerprints and explicit invalidators; require expected outputs and their current integrity before avoiding work.

**Semantic disposition.** Existing dependency/currentness and exact-reuse relations. Mutable completion markers have no event-admission, replay, continuation or worksite-truth authority.

**Realisation disposition.** Adapt cache-key and artifact-check mechanics below the relevant owner. Optimise/local-implement pure construction reuse where exact contracts match; consume ABG recovery and continuation rather than substituting Harness markers.

**Preserved identities, effects and authority.** Definition/input/schema/model/configuration and declared dependency basis; actual producer and current use; effect status, before/after observations and residue; original allowances, causal prefix and continuation phase. Hash equality alone grants no authority.

**Plausible benefit, unmeasured.** Potentially avoid unnecessary reconstruction/calls and detect stale experimental results, unmeasured.

**Next decisive test.** Exercise an invalidation matrix: input/context, artifact, definition, schema, model/endpoint/tool profile, grant, workspace dependency, pending phase and expected prefix changes; include missing/partial output, interrupted owner and uncertain effects. Verify reuse only where declared law permits it.

**Limits.** Harness model compatibility also relies on experiment configuration; its baseline marker is not a full environment digest. WorkBuddy lacks the optional output-integrity hook used by JobBench/LAB. Mutable hashes/markers are not adversarial custody or ABG exact recovery.

## L05 — Reproducible comparative protocol and complete accounting

**Sources/counterparts:** [H01](harness-algorithms.md#h01), [H17](harness-algorithms.md#h17), [H19](harness-algorithms.md#h19) → [A10](abg-algorithms.md#a10), [A18](abg-algorithms.md#a18), [A22](abg-algorithms.md#a22), [A23](abg-algorithms.md#a23).

**Precise mechanism.** Declare task streams, experiment cells, nominal trial allocation, actual attempts, failed/ungraded coverage and comparable metrics; retain the original acceptance population and independent scoring basis.

**Semantic disposition.** Existing measurement/assessment relations plus a concrete comparative experiment protocol. Harness allocation constants are policy facts, not general ABG resource or retry law.

**Realisation disposition.** Adapt/local-implement experiment manifests and accounting below existing measurement/proof owners; consume their result/assessment admission. Accounting is a prerequisite to interpreting a comparison, not a new controller.

**Preserved identities, effects and authority.** Exact subject, task/model/permission/evidence populations, original criteria, actual attempts including failures/recovery, score units, denominator and causal producer. Do not convert ungraded exclusions into evidence of high whole-population quality.

**Plausible benefit, unmeasured.** Potentially fairer decisions about quality versus cost and less confounded experimentation; advantage is unmeasured.

**Next decisive test.** Before comparing outcome quality, reconcile nominal jobs with actual solver, improver, selector, grader, retry and recovery requests; capture input/output/cache tokens, spend, work/CPU where material, wall time and result coverage. Use matched actual budgets and retain per-task failure/ungraded outcomes.

**Limits.** Harness nominal structure is 4k rollout jobs and 3k improvement stages per mode, or 7k rollout jobs for both modes sharing k baselines. These are not measured physical model-call counts: caches, resume, early failure and retries change actual calls. Current CLI dollars cover improver stages/selection/reflection, not all solver/grader costs. No hard aggregate financial budget is established.

## L06 — Concrete candidate-edit diagnostics and bounded repair

**Sources/counterparts:** [H03](harness-algorithms.md#h03), [H04](harness-algorithms.md#h04) → [A01](abg-algorithms.md#a01), [A04](abg-algorithms.md#a04), [A14](abg-algorithms.md#a14).

**Precise mechanism.** Validate candidate edit paths, declarations, referenced helpers and supported syntax/schema; return specific diagnostic feedback for a separately bounded correction attempt before admission.

**Semantic disposition.** Consume existing closed mechanical checks wherever their meaning matches. Harness tree/syntax checks are not GTL whole-Program validity, semantic applicability or effect authorization; new checks need their own owner/applicability.

**Realisation disposition.** Share an equivalent closed checker or implement/adapt it locally for substrate/dependency reasons. Preserve the original semantic owner rather than owning a copied authoritative relation.

**Preserved identities, effects and authority.** Candidate versus admitted value, declared editable territory, expected source/target contracts, permitted capability/effects, original failed artifact/diagnostics and retry identity/allowance. Repair feedback does not silently authorize another effect or erase performed work.

**Plausible benefit, unmeasured.** Potentially fewer malformed proposals and clearer actor feedback, unmeasured on ABG's actual context.

**Next decisive test.** Feed missing dependencies, off-surface edits, malformed but parseable carriers, unsupported hooks and semantically inapplicable valid shapes. Confirm useful diagnostics, authorised attempt bounds and refusal before disallowed admission/effects.

**Limits.** Syntax compilation and closed schema checks prove only those closed relations. Automatically adjusting trigger text does not prove skill relevance or safety; F_P suitability and Product/owner checks remain separate.

## L07 — Selection presentation without forced-adoption semantics

**Sources/counterparts:** [H09](harness-algorithms.md#h09), [H10](harness-algorithms.md#h10) → [A09](abg-algorithms.md#a09), [A11](abg-algorithms.md#a11), [A14](abg-algorithms.md#a14), [A22](abg-algorithms.md#a22), [A23](abg-algorithms.md#a23).

**Precise mechanism.** Borrow clear candidate eligibility, readable comparison presentation, membership parsing and explicit selector-failure diagnostics. Treat final-pool policy separately from the user outcome/acceptance contract.

**Semantic disposition.** Existing choice, candidate/result admission and assessment/closure relations. Harness finals-only and single-final direct delivery are not adopted as default ABG acceptance law. Any different choice policy is a deliberate adaptation, not equivalence to the Harness policy.

**Realisation disposition.** Adapt the parser/presentation/diagnostic machinery conditionally. Consume ABG hold/gap/refusal, independent judgment, baseline comparison or other alternatives where the owning contract requires them.

**Preserved identities, effects and authority.** Declared permissible choices, original evidence/criteria, explicit missing support and unsuccessful judgments, assessor independence, parent reevaluation and exact closure. No universal baseline-comparison obligation is invented here.

**Plausible benefit, unmeasured.** Potentially clearer comparison decisions with fewer invalid labels; quality benefit is unmeasured. This is a conditional, lower-priority candidate rather than a requirement to rebuild selection.

**Next decisive test.** Include all-worse candidates, a sole delivered but wrong final, empty output, invalid identifier, selector failure and required unresolved criteria. Check that the owning contract's hold/refusal/alternatives remain available and no failed selector becomes accepted work.

**Limits.** Harness deliberately excludes baselines/abstention and directly delivers an eligible sole final at k=1. Its failure fallback remains unsuccessful. Those facts prevent treating adoption as proof of improvement or enclosing acceptance.

## L08 — Execution adapters and bounded scratch sessions

**Sources/counterparts:** [H15](harness-algorithms.md#h15), [H18](harness-algorithms.md#h18) → [A06](abg-algorithms.md#a06), [A10](abg-algorithms.md#a10), [A14](abg-algorithms.md#a14), [A18](abg-algorithms.md#a18), [A21](abg-algorithms.md#a21).

**Precise mechanism.** Use fresh scratch/session state, explicit native installation/materialization, concurrency limits, timeout/backoff and adapter-specific trace extraction under declared leaf/effect owners.

**Semantic disposition.** Existing invocation/effect/attempt relations. Replacing Python by TypeScript is an execution-binding choice, not a new calculus or proof that all environment behavior is preserved.

**Realisation disposition.** Adapt or deliberately implement locally for isolation, substrate, dependency ownership and maintenance reasons. Consume existing owner/adapter interfaces; a new tenant is unnecessary merely to assess the mechanism.

**Preserved identities, effects and authority.** Exact actor/call/input/harness basis, declared territory and capability, retry class/allowance, before/after observations, actual termination, possible partial effects/residue, result provenance and admission. Native agent internal strategy does not become runtime authority.

**Plausible benefit, unmeasured.** Potentially simpler integration or reduced accidental session carryover, unmeasured. Include adapter maintenance, licences, third-party benchmark/dependency setup and transport cost.

**Next decisive test.** Check supported native bindings under controlled grants with interruption, hanging descendant, stale before-state, partial artifact, failed harvest, duplicate request and scope violation cases. Verify after-state/residue and truthful refusal independently of process exit or response parseability.

**Limits.** Unrestricted network and full-access/disabled-approval execution are not security confinement. OpenCode path fences and fresh local state are bounded controls. No security or operational equivalence is established by reusing launcher code.

## Relations with no Harness authoritative equivalent

ABG's [A15](abg-algorithms.md#a15) event admission, [A16](abg-algorithms.md#a16) Event Calculus, [A17](abg-algorithms.md#a17) exact recovery, [A19](abg-algorithms.md#a19) continuation, [A22](abg-algorithms.md#a22) proof acquisition and [A23](abg-algorithms.md#a23) scope/Run closure are not supplied by a Harness file, marker, trace, learned archive or successful process. Those artifacts can be candidate evidence at an existing owner seam. They do not author ABG truth or discharge independent assessment.

This does not mean every mechanism must be duplicated, nor that ABG has already qualified every current implementation. Generic hashing, filtering, materialisation and indexing can be reused where meaning and operational law match. Current source and candidate design remain distinct from accepted installed behavior. No rewrite or new tenant is required to assess the policy loans.

## Concrete next comparison design — proposed, not run

1. Bind one granted frozen workflow, original Product outcome and independent acceptance criteria. Select repeated held-out tasks and the tested population before seeing results.
2. Compare the existing ABG policy with a precisely declared Harness-derived policy over the same ABG workflow/owners. An optional native-Harness arm can help isolate execution-binding overhead; it must not silently change the task, outcome or authority.
3. Hold tasks, actual input/source bodies, model versions/settings, permitted tools/network, effect territory, evidence/assessment access and failure handling constant. Match actual total resource budgets rather than nominal k alone.
4. Retain every attempt, failure, refusal, partial artifact and ungraded assessment, with their real denominators. Separate framework processing, solver/improver/selector/grader/recovery work, tokens, spend and wall time. Preserve unknown costs as unknown.
5. Obtain independent assessment against the original criteria, not an improver's self-judgment or only the chosen artifact. Report per-task quality, correct completion/coverage, regression, overhead and uncertainty across repetitions.
6. Stop or reject under the selected grant and declared failure/resource limits; do not retry until green. Keep original bases, effects, admitted provenance and closure semantics intact. No runtime generation/publication of new GTL is admitted by this proposal.

Such a trial could establish whether a specific policy helps on a stated workload. It would not establish universal superiority, semantic equivalence of every framework relation, whole-Product qualification or release acceptance. No trial, model call, implementation or new acceptance was performed in this report.
