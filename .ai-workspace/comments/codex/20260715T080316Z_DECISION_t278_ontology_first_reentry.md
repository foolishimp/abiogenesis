# Decision - T-278 Ontology-First Re-Entry

**Date**: 2026-07-15

**Status**: active re-entry authorized; Ontology acceptance pending
**Authority**: direct F_H instruction to revise Design Module Method with the
Ontology prior and continue under the new constitution

## Decision

The ABIogenesis 5.0 public operation wave re-enters at PRODUCT before further
operation realization.

The current 36-operation roster was produced by identifying required
functionality and promoting each named behavior into a peer public operation.
Prime contraction later reduced duplicate realization surfaces, but it did not
question whether the operation family itself had been derived from the product
entities, lifecycle, authority, and composition algebra. Under the revised
Design Module Method, that derivation is mandatory and prior.

T-278 is the singular `product_reprice` carrier. The current 36 behaviors remain
a no-silence input. Their exact operation identities and count are not presumed
to survive.

## Boundary

- Preserve existing dirty T-270/T-272 work; do not unwind it by inference.
- Do not checkpoint that work as closure under the old operation basis.
- Do not implement the 17 missing roster entries.
- Author the candidate public control-plane Ontology and submit it for explicit
  F_H review.
- Do not treat this re-entry authorization as acceptance of the candidate
  Ontology.
- After acceptance, propagate one truth through PRODUCT, requirements, goals,
  T-244, active tickets, designs, public contracts, realization, and proof.
- Preserve no compatibility facade for non-derived operation identities.

## Reason

The product already has GTL and ABG ontologies and an explicit monadic compute
algebra. The missing prior is the public control-plane Ontology that maps
product entities and interaction goals into parameterized functions and
higher-order compositions over those existing laws. Without that prior,
operation count and API shape are accidental design authority.
