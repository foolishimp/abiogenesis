## Review

The design maps every S05 authority to an existing accepted boundary:

- one closed Product-owned Consensus domain family;
- one ABIogenesis-owned ModulePublication and admitted Program;
- one canonical SYSTEM GraphFunction plus subordinate declared callable
  members;
- direct GTL fan-out, fan-in, bounded recursion, foldback, and F_H;
- existing validator, HoG, implementation-port, ABG event/replay,
  continuation, SDK, and CLI paths; and
- one source-blind driver applying the same public contract to three explicit
  workspace bindings.

Agreement, recursive dispute, and unresolved escalation remain distinct.
Only unresolved `escalate_fh` enters the ordinary F_H helper. The driver may
transport a Product-declared escalation target but owns no panel, round,
reducer, event, continuation, or closure behavior.

The three semantic views and IACS preserve the accepted authority split.
Prime contraction leaves one domain authoring family, one GTL publication,
and one ABG runtime authority. Historical X schema registries,
native-definition joins, operation rosters, runners, and projectors remain
excluded.

The design explicitly holds S05 closure until the canonical callable is also
reached through Program-owned One Surface selection. A first direct-callable
implementation cut may establish the core without claiming S05.
