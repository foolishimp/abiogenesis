# T-255 Design Review - Compiled GraphVector Execution Handoff

## Verdict

Accepted for incremental realization under the direct F_H continuation ruling,
with one proportional reprice. The former retrospective design did not cover
T-255's current boundary and is replaced by a current three-view design.

## Findings

1. The old design froze the GraphFunction-wide HoG handoff while explicitly
   excluding exact GraphVector program selection, generalized flat C shape,
   target/closure contracts, and application-lineage composition ownership.
   Extending it would preserve the defect T-255 exists to remove.
2. The T-252 body proves the generic carrier split: 35 materialized vectors,
   34 exact T-254 bindings, 28 flat lowerable programs, five workflow terms,
   one retry term, and one selector-free structural HOF wrapper.
3. Fifteen applied-host vectors cannot use the ordinary composition resolver
   because their declarations correctly retain operand ownership. T-255 must
   consume T-265 lineage and provisional owner projections rather than rewrite
   those declarations onto the applied host.
4. The broad `traversal_execution_contracts` family cannot close inside T-255.
   Plugin result-interface and bind-conservation truth require later admitted
   authorities, and the structural HOF wrapper intentionally has no C selector.
   T-267 is the singular final owner.
5. Effect compatibility remains behind the exact DS-4 capability profile. The
   handoff may retain effect requirements and an explicit deferred status but
   cannot infer compatibility from refs, plugins, package versions, or tests.

## Accepted Carrier

`CompiledGraphVectorExecutionHandoff` is one derived M03 carrier joining the
unchanged T-254 binding, admitted flat C program or identity disposition,
effective composition and owner lineage, exact target-carrier contract, and a
derived edge-closure contract projection. It is runtime-addressable but is not
a GTL declaration, runtime closure verdict, or second selector.

## Proof Expectations

- exact direct and inherited composition-owner joins;
- one-stage, multi-stage, and identity non-Consensus fixtures;
- unchanged workflow/retry/HOF successor gaps;
- target/closure projection for every T-252 vector;
- runtime consumption with no GraphFunction-global selector fallback;
- unchanged T-252 body digest;
- module, full semantic, packed/publication, and Mermaid gates.
