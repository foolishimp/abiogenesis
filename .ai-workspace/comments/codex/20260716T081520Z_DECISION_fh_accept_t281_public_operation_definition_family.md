# F_H Decision - Accept T-281 Public Operation Definition Family

Accept the independently reviewed T-281 design at final status-projected digest
`2b5153aedb06dc5c814bf356de45b1ec5bc3b91a766d107002d0f2b3176e6f6e`
for P1 implementation.

The accepted boundary authors one closed 19-operation definition family and
derives native types, raw admission, schemas, catalog rows, SDK declarations,
CLI coordinates, and parity inventories from that family. It does not move
semantic behavior into metadata or publish a partial product surface.

P2 handler binding and packed catalog, SDK, CLI, capability, source/package,
and clean-install parity remain separately gated and release-blocking.
