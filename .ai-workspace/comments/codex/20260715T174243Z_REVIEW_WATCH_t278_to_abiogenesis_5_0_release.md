The current 27-atom claim is not yet proved. The Ontology classifies
`ExactCandidateQualification<K>` as a Prime contract family with independent
qualification authority, immutable basis/verdict lifecycle, and release-gating
effect, but assigns construction and admission of its basis and verdict to an
unnamed "subordinate join/fold" inside the qualify-release composition
(`ABIOGENESIS_PUBLIC_CONTROL_PLANE_ONTOLOGY.md` lines 1074, 1194, and 1371).

That is not composition of the declared atomic family as currently written:

- the accepted C algebra sequences declared atomic `C.of` leaves; it does not
  manufacture an unlisted semantic evaluator or admission boundary;
- `AF-03` is a projection from authoritative source truth and cannot silently
  become the authority that admits the qualification verdict; and
- Design Module Method Prime law identifies a new validation or admission
  boundary as a typical lawful reason for a Prime function.

The candidate must make one coherent choice before the atom count can be
accepted:

1. promote the exact-candidate basis/verdict evaluator-admitter as a named
   atomic function and recompute the Prime count; or
2. prove the verdict is only a subordinate deterministic projection of already
   admitted owning verdicts, remove its independent qualification/admission
   authority, and make `AF-25` the named verifier of the complete projection.

The requirement that qualification aggregate owning proofs rather than create a
second semantic checker does not make the aggregation computation disappear.
It constrains that computation to completeness, basis, bypass, and disposition
folding over existing verdicts.

### Confirmed Finding - RW-002 - P1

The final-tap relation remains under-specified. `REQ-P-QUAL-068` requires the RC
record to bind the allowed version/release-asset delta. `REQ-P-QUAL-070` permits
only the final version and reconciled release-scoped assets to differ from the
accepted RC and requires affected gates to rerun. T-248 narrates those facts,
but the Ontology still defines:

```text
AF-25 materializeReleaseCut(exactBasis, exactVerdict, releaseIdentity)
```

There is no exact carrier for the accepted-RC source, allowed final delta,
delta classification, affected-gate set, rerun outcomes, or reconciliation
verdict. Therefore `AF-25(tapped_release, ...)` cannot prove that changed final
bytes are the only allowed changes or that they remain attributable to the
qualified RC.

This does not necessarily require another atomic function. It does require an
explicit typed final-tap transition basis and verifier input owned by `AF-25`,
plus negative cases for behavior, declaration, contract, dependency, or
unclassified asset changes that must reopen the RC window.
