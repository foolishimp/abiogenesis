# abiogenesis 4.6.0-rc.3 Release Candidate Note

It follows `4.6.0-rc.2` and publishes the current 4.6 candidate as one
immutable downstream-consumable cut. This is an RC publication, not the final
4.6.0 tap.

## Retained 4.6 substrate

- The deterministic witness, operator, observer, and tuner substrate delivered
  under T-217 remains in the candidate.
- Declared plugin selection resolves governed seam references through the
  standard catalog. Standard live F_P dispatch and evaluation remain
  capability-gated, and plugin admission checks kind and driver compatibility
  before invocation.
- The finite R5 repair gate is retained: canonical C-call identity, idempotent
  invocation bundles, post-launch evidence, confined artifact paths, and
  capability provenance are covered by the current semantic proof lane.
- T-219's specification reconciliation records the realized event, witness,
  payload, plugin-seam, qualification, and operator-contract law without
  turning implementation detail into product ontology.

## Typed GTL and C authoring boundary

- T-220 publishes the seven-term C authoring algebra: `C.of`, `C.id`,
  `C.compose`, `C.edge`, `workflow.C`, `C.batch`, and `C.retry`.
- Native TypeScript types and construction APIs reject locally decidable GTL
  category errors. Raw admission applies the same structural judgments to
  serialized declarations.
- The semantic compiler reports unresolved references, illegal hosts,
  malformed or duplicate declarations, catalog and membership failures, and
  lawful-but-unrealized constructs with stable diagnostics.
- The runtime consumes the compiled execution-declaration handoff rather than
  reinterpreting raw declaration bags.
- Malformed, incomplete, or contradictory F_P responses are rejected or made
  non-close at response admission.

## Exact-cut evidence

The versioned release snapshot is the exact-cut proof carrier. Its manifest
records the clean source commit, semantic build, semantic lint, full semantic
suite summary, package identity, packed tarball identity, checksums, and this
RC note. Downstream qualification must consume that tarball rather than a
mutable source path.

## Predecessor lineage

The clean data-mapper campaign recorded for rc.2 remains valid evidence for
the rc.2 path that actually ran. It is predecessor lineage only. It does not
prove declarations-only odd_glc adoption or a post-adoption campaign on rc.3.

## Explicit exclusions

- T-217 did not complete its original feature ticket. Its retained substrate
  closed through a superseded-and-split disposition with named successors.
- Declarations-only odd_glc adoption, the one-schema downstream worker result,
  the 11.5B audit, and the full data-mapper rerun remain odd_glc T-033 work.
- `workflow.C`, `C.batch`, and `C.retry` are lawful authored terms, but their
  complete runtime realization is not claimed by this cut.
- Live observer/tuner campaign closure, executable Review/Consensus
  composition, sticky sessions, and integrated zero-out-of-framework proof are
  not claimed.
- C-2 monolith splitting and C-6 barrel pruning are not release claims.
- ABG 5.0 catalog, graph shell, marketplace compatibility, public consumption,
  and self-hosting claims are outside this RC.

## Operating boundary

This RC targets one trusted developer desktop. Its defensive boundary is
malformed authored GTL and malformed, incomplete, or contradictory F_P output.
It does not claim hostile local-object, filesystem-tamper, hosted multi-tenant,
or marketplace-service hardening.
