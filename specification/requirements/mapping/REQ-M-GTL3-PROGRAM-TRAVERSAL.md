# REQ-M-GTL3-PROGRAM-TRAVERSAL - GTL, HoG, And ABG Mapping

**Status**: T-283 constitutional candidate
**Category**: Capability
**Date**: 2026-07-20
**Derives from**: [INTENT.md](../../INTENT.md), [PRODUCT.md](../../PRODUCT.md), [REQ-L-GTL3-GRAPHFUNCTION.md](../gtl/REQ-L-GTL3-GRAPHFUNCTION.md), [REQ-R-ABG3-INTERPRET.md](../abg/REQ-R-ABG3-INTERPRET.md)

## Purpose

Define the lossless authority mapping from authored GTL through non-lowering
validation, direct HoG traversal, and ABG runtime truth. This family prevents a
workspace, adapter, compiler projection, implementation binding, or runtime
helper from becoming a rival program or executor.

## Acceptance Criteria

**REQ-M-GTL3-PROGRAM-TRAVERSAL-001**: GTL program, GraphFunction, materialized
Graph, workspace, HoG invocation, and ABG runtime episode shall remain distinct.
A GTL graph composition is the program. GraphFunction is its sole named
callable work contract. A workspace supplies mutable instance state. HoG
traverses. ABG admits runtime truth.

**REQ-M-GTL3-PROGRAM-TRAVERSAL-002**: A GTL program shall own topology,
program starts, callable membership, roles, compute composition, policy refs,
effect and result contracts, proof obligations, and publication metadata. It
shall not emit runtime events or acquire truth merely by being authored.

**REQ-M-GTL3-PROGRAM-TRAVERSAL-003**: A workspace shall provide files, data,
configuration, observations, generated artifacts, and run archives. It shall
not become program authority, callable-selection truth, traversal state,
closure truth, or a controller.

**REQ-M-GTL3-PROGRAM-TRAVERSAL-004**: Public execution shall bind exact Product,
install, workspace, catalog view, admitted program, GraphFunction membership,
contracts, and invocation authority before HoG enters the selected graph.

**REQ-M-GTL3-PROGRAM-TRAVERSAL-005**: TypeScript and the GTL validator shall
judge local and whole-program law before effect. Their outputs may include
canonical serialization, typed diagnostics, normalized indexes, and unresolved
semantic pressure. They shall not be executable traversal input independent of
the admitted GTL value.

**REQ-M-GTL3-PROGRAM-TRAVERSAL-006**: HoG shall traverse the original admitted
GTL program and materialized GraphFunction graphs directly. It shall not
require a generated HoG program, CompiledCProgramPlan, compiled execution
declaration, runtime-program catalog, or other executable intermediate
representation.

**REQ-M-GTL3-PROGRAM-TRAVERSAL-007**: Deterministic, probabilistic, human,
worker, tool, and process execution shall occur inside declared traversal
boundaries. An effect implementation shall not own topology, traversal bind,
runtime admission, continuation, or closure.

**REQ-M-GTL3-PROGRAM-TRAVERSAL-008**: ABG runtime truth shall preserve at least
the exact program, callable, materialized graph, graph call, traversal locus,
frame and span lineage, workspace binding, selected compute regime, effect
invocation, result and evidence admission, consequence, judgment, continuation,
and replay projection.

**REQ-M-GTL3-PROGRAM-TRAVERSAL-009**: A GraphFunction shall not be relabeled as
the whole product program. It remains reusable, bindable, composable,
substitutable, refinable, and callable only through admitted program and catalog
membership.

**REQ-M-GTL3-PROGRAM-TRAVERSAL-010**: Slot maps, lifecycle maps, catalogs,
dashboards, parity matrices, validation indexes, and runtime caches shall be
derived projections unless independently admitted by their owning authority.
They shall not select traversal or become program state.

**REQ-M-GTL3-PROGRAM-TRAVERSAL-011**: A public start or GraphFunction call shall
enter through the installed public admission path. A CLI, SDK, workspace file,
fixture, plugin, or direct vector call shall not select a private program,
worker, topology, or execution basis.

**REQ-M-GTL3-PROGRAM-TRAVERSAL-012**: Downstream products may publish modules,
programs, GraphFunctions, types, contracts, and implementation bindings through
the common catalog law. They shall not publish a parallel executor, registry,
event writer, continuation model, or closure ledger.

**REQ-M-GTL3-PROGRAM-TRAVERSAL-013**: Design and realization shall identify the
owner and source of truth for declaration, validation, admission, traversal,
effect execution, event admission, replay, continuation, and projection. Any
unowned join is a typed design gap.

**REQ-M-GTL3-PROGRAM-TRAVERSAL-014**: An execution proof shall enter through an
admitted program start or GraphFunction call and shall cite ABG replay for every
traversal-affecting result. Direct calls to vectors, plugins, workers, or
fixtures are not parity proof.

**REQ-M-GTL3-PROGRAM-TRAVERSAL-015**: Installed context and bootloader
projections shall bind their selected Product and method identities and shall
be checked for stale wording that assigns program, traversal, or closure
authority to GraphFunctions, workspaces, adapters, or ABG as executor.

**REQ-M-GTL3-PROGRAM-TRAVERSAL-016**: The GTL validator and context-conformance
checks shall report or reject drift. They shall not write instruction files,
mutate workspace binding, install Products, select a program, emit runtime
events, or replace the Product-owned materialization function.
