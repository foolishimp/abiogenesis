## Purpose

ABG governs derived artifacts (bootloader, self-description) under the same replay/provenance discipline as all other graph applications.

## Acceptance Criteria

**REQ-R-ABG2-SELFHOSTING-001**: Derived graph artifacts (bootloader documents, constraint surfaces) shall be governed by the same convergence, replay, and provenance discipline as all other graph work.

**REQ-R-ABG2-SELFHOSTING-002**: Drift between source-of-truth graph/materials and derived artifacts shall be detectable through deterministic consistency checks.

**REQ-R-ABG2-SELFHOSTING-003**: Derived artifact governance is a graph application — not special bootstrap magic.
