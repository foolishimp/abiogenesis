## Purpose

Define the ABG side of hook resolution, runtime admissibility, and configured
default bundles.

## Acceptance Criteria

**REQ-R-ABG3-POLICY-001**: GTL shall declare hook attachment points, stable hook references, and opaque configuration. HoG shall reach those hooks through the admitted program; implementation binding resolves the interior behavior, and ABG admits its result and effects.

**REQ-R-ABG3-POLICY-002**: ABG shall govern admissible regimes, fallback law, proof, closure, and observability boundaries. It shall not define or own internal strategy for probabilistic workers.

**REQ-R-ABG3-POLICY-003**: ABG shall ship broad reference default bundles as ordinary configuration plus executable hook implementations, not hidden hardcoded law tables.

**REQ-R-ABG3-POLICY-004**: Domain users shall be able to copy, edit, and reference default bundles from their own GTL/ABG surfaces.

**REQ-R-ABG3-POLICY-005**: ABG shall fail closed on unresolved hook references, malformed config, or illegal resolved policy bundles.

**REQ-R-ABG3-POLICY-006**: ABG shall publish supervised probabilistic dispatch as a first-class runtime capability. It remains automated runtime truth, not disguised human review.

**REQ-R-ABG3-POLICY-007**: ABIogenesis may publish a root-level supervision policy selected by an admitted system GTL program for run-wide observation and recovery. The policy shall not create an imperative control plane around repeated `start`, and it shall remain distinct from per-edge dispatch capability and the traversal request grammar.

**REQ-R-ABG3-POLICY-008**: ABG.Fn composition defaults, templates, fallback policy, standards context, and optimization policy shall resolve from visible configuration or published GTL assets. Hidden code constants, null contracts, prompt prose, parser convention, current process cwd, and replay-time filesystem discovery shall not satisfy composition policy authority.
