## Local Extension: Mutable-Worksite Causality

### F-WORKSITE-CAUSALITY

**Evaluation family.** Determine whether one mutable-worksite change has a
complete authority, effect, observation, admission, and replay relation.

**Basis.** Exact `WorkspaceAuthorityBasis`, ProductSet, resolved lock, declared
roots, stable `WorkspaceBinding`, Program, GraphFunction and C locus,
`ExecutionBasis`, predecessor `ObservationSnapshot`, operation grant, mutation
subject, write territory, handler binding, ABG predecessor prefix, and work
authority.

**Material relations.** Admitted execution, intention, and invocation basis;
declared GTL GraphFunction effect; HoG locus and binding selection; effect
owner; physical mutation and receipt; successor observation; semantic-owner
candidate; ABG event and causal predecessor; dependent-projection
invalidation; Event Calculus and fresh replay.

**Coordinate law.** A content change under the same `WorkspaceAuthorityBasis`,
ProductSet, resolved lock, and declared roots preserves `WorkspaceBinding` and
creates a new observation. A change to a binding constituent requires a new
binding and its owning re-entry. A Catalog, view, Program, or traversal change
may require a new `ExecutionBasis` without changing the binding.

**Invariants.** GTL owns effect meaning; HoG derives a candidate; the declared
owner performs the effect; ABG alone admits runtime truth; replay reconstructs
it. Worksite observations are immutable evidence but replaceable as current
inputs.

**Authority.** The frame evaluates only. Existing Product, GraphFunction,
effect-owner, ABG, and Executive authorities retain their decisions.

**Evidence.** Exact predecessor observation, operation grant and write
territory, effect request, HoG-selected locus, owner result or refusal,
physical receipt, successor observation, semantic-owner candidate, admitted
ABG event, and fresh replay or reconstruction.

**Exclusions.** `WorkspaceBinding` as content state; private filesystem reads
as runtime truth; unadmitted mutation; stale observation reuse; rebinding after
ordinary content change; fixture-authored events or closure; or semantic
rollback inferred from physical compensation.

**Invalidation.** `WorkspaceAuthorityBasis`, ProductSet, resolved lock,
declared root, `ExecutionBasis`, graph topology, operation grant, write
territory, effect contract, evidence basis, or capability-envelope change
invalidates the activation or requires the declared re-entry.

**Capability envelope.** Competence in workspace authority, filesystem or
tool effects, immutable observation, ABG admission, causal history, and replay.
An assurance activation also requires independence from authorship or repair of
the exact candidate.

**Dependencies.** ABIogenesis, GTL, HoG, ABG, Effect, Owner, and Proof frame
results may contribute evidence through declared source routes. No dependency
transfers its authority.

**Result.** Return only the shared Reference Frame Method closed evaluation
result: frame and activation identity; exact subject and basis; actor and
capability envelope; semantic, evaluation, operation where material, and
decision authority; admissible evidence and inference; exactly one of
`satisfied`, `falsified`, `indeterminate`, `out_of_frame`, or `invalid_basis`;
residual uncertainty; invalidation conditions; and consuming conjunction or
decision. Product candidates, observations, receipts, events, prefixes, and
replay projections are bound subject or evidence coordinates, never outputs
authored by this evaluation frame.

## Activation And Return

Activation, Worker/Reviewer return, result conjunction, and revision follow the
selected STDO baseline. A sufficient direct Writer activation uses the existing
work authority and result carrier; coordination is selected only for material
contexts, authority, capability or required independence. Each bounded
assignment shows its frame URI, purpose, subject, basis, source routes,
existing authority, permitted effects, evidence, stops and declared result
consumer; that consumer is Executive in the coordinated branch.

The ordinary causal conjunction is:

```text
stable WorkspaceBinding + predecessor observation
  -> GTL-declared effect candidate
  -> owner-performed mutation or refusal
  -> successor observation or residue
  -> ABG admission
  -> fresh replay-derived ObservationSnapshot
  -> next graph boundary
```

The Executive may use overlapping results without merging internal contexts.
Conjunction preserves conflicts and residuals; it does not select a convenient
green result or transfer authority.

## Executive, Writer, And Reviewer Binding

The STDO engagement roles remain exactly Executive, Worker, and Reviewer.
`Writer` is the local name for a Worker activation whose operation grant permits
bounded source mutation. It is not a fourth role and acquires no Product,
admission, acceptance, or publication authority.

This configuration permits direct Writer entry under the selected
`STDO_REFERENCE_FRAME_BASELINE.md#engagement-applicability`. One capable
context with sufficient intake and an exact existing operation grant needs no
Executive appointment solely to begin. Materially distinct contexts and any
required independence select coordination. The RC6 migration is coordinated
under its recorded separate Writer grants and independent assessment; direct
entry does not waive that specific requirement or the held C0 design gate.

`REFERENCE_FRAME_METHOD.md#applicability-and-required-results` owns required
evaluations: true applicability requires the result, false excludes only that
obligation, and unknown withholds the dependent claim. A listed frame, role,
tool or review event alone creates no requirement and supplies no closure.
Valid computed facts, recorded judgments and original owner rulings are reused
through their source routes under SPEC_METHOD and TICKET_METHOD; only material
invalidators reopen affected support. Independent assessment supplies its own
judgment and cannot reuse the author's judgment as independent assurance.

In the coordinated branch, Executive owns attention management, evaluation scheduling, priority,
promotion-boundary effect, disposition, and the next already-authorized action.
It presents one reconstructable activation per coherent subject, consumes only
closed returns, preserves conflicts and residuals, and conjoins results only at
declared seams. A Writer returns changed paths, verification evidence,
residuals, and the exact Executive return route. A Reviewer evaluates a frozen
subject independently, assigns technical severity and triage, does not edit,
and returns to the Executive.

Executive performs no candidate, Product or worktree mutation. A same-actor
transition to Writer is explicit and recorded before the exact granted effect;
the actor is not Executive for that effect. Tool access and a small, reversible
or test-only change create no exception. Direct Writer completion records only
satisfied applicable conditions within its grant; reserved acceptance and
required independent assessment remain with their existing owners.

The project severity scale is descending:

- `S0`: catastrophic or non-waivable authority, integrity, safety, or data-loss
  failure;
- `S1`: mandatory Product outcome or hard-stop failure without an adequate
  workaround;
- `S2`: material semantic, authority, causal, proportionality, or proof defect
  with a bounded workaround;
- `S3`: minor ambiguity or evidence defect without loss of a mandatory outcome;
- `S4`: editorial or cosmetic defect; and
- `not_applicable` or `indeterminate`: no supported technical grade.

The Executive priority scale is descending `P0` through `P5`. `P0` through
`P2` block the current MVP boundary until repaired, explicitly narrowed out of
the current claim, or retained by an authority allowed to accept that risk.
`P3` through `P5` are retained but do not block the current boundary. No
priority mapping may waive an STDO hard stop, fabricate missing Reviewer
assessment, or turn an adjacent out-of-claim observation into a blocker.

The selected method's steel-thread law orders the smallest real runnable
Product path early, then extends it by consequential uncertainty, dependency,
interface reach and failure impact. A passing path proves only what it
exercises. Focused regression and judgment probes address construction
uncertainty; substantial qualification cycles occur at their applicable
capability or release boundary. Existing lawful threads and valid evidence are
reused; remaining refusal, recovery and interaction obligations are retained.
This is selected STDO law, not an ABI-local candidate for shared-method
promotion. It grants no activation of the held Wave 2 repair streams.

For a coordinated current-MVP increment, the activation relation is:

```text
exact Product mandate and current candidate
  -> smallest dependency-ready Writer activation
  -> construction and focused evidence on the selected steel thread
  -> independent Reviewer over the frozen delta where required
  -> Executive priority and boundary disposition
  -> next already-authorized increment | bounded repair | stop and re-enter
```

Requests state role and outcome, exact subject and frame, hard constraints,
material evidence and source routes, open solution space, return and stop
contract, then `ACTION` last. This is a structured-prose projection, not a
prompt engine. Anything not prohibited by authority or the activation remains
available to the capable actor.
