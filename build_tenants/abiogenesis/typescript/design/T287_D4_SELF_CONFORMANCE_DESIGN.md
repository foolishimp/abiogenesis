## Evaluation Relation

1. Admit the exact qualification and law basis, catalog, inventory and tenant
   manifest. Resolve required bytes and native evidence coordinates. Missing,
   stale, conflicting, unreadable or cross-subject truth yields a typed
   non-green diagnostic naming the affected input; never reuse a prior verdict.
2. Join rule applications to their published source and inventory subjects.
   Apply only the declared finite conformance relations. Check completeness and
   uniqueness; unknown applicability remains explicit. There is no generic
   prose classifier or caller-supplied executable checker.
3. Evaluate computable relations through existing raw admission, GTL validator
   and declared native conformance rules. Resolve required admitted semantic
   assessments and owner decisions without replacing their judgment.
4. Construct all findings and their dispositions, retaining residuals and
   original failure evidence. Derive one same-basis gate result. It performs no
   edits, repairs, gate scheduling or qualification/release reduction.

| Authority | Establishes | Cannot establish |

## Findings, Result And Qualification Join

The self-conformance result binds subject-basis ref/digest, law-basis ref/digest,
catalog identity/version/digest, inventory digest, exact rule applications,
findings, evidence refs/digests, affected surface refs and overall disposition.
Each finding identifies its rule/source, subject, evaluated premises, C/J/O
provenance, diagnostic and disposition. Identity is the canonical content digest
of those immutable fields, excluding its own resulting ref/digest. Changed
subject, law, application, evidence or ruling creates a different result.

Findings distinguish `passed`, `failed`, `inapplicable_with_reason`,
`blocked_incomplete` and `accepted_reentry`. An inapplicability/exclusion must
resolve its permitted authority. Accepted re-entry records actual admitted work
and retains the unsatisfied obligation; a ticket label alone is insufficient.
It cannot make an applicable unfinished obligation green.

The deterministic gate fold preserves every finding: a failed obligation yields
failed; otherwise unresolved mandatory work/applicability/evidence yields
blocked/incomplete; passed requires complete inventory and a sufficient terminal
disposition for every applicable required surface/rule. F11 cannot be excluded
wholesale for an ABIogenesis 5.0 qualification subject. The gate result is not
an `ExactCandidateQualification<verdict>`.

QUAL-064's existing native structural admission consumes this owning result as
one member of the complete ordered `QualificationGateResultVector<K>`:
passed maps to green, failed to red and unresolved to blocked, preserving exact
basis, result/evidence identities and bypass facts. It cannot reinterpret the
finding or replace a missing gate. Exactly one separate declared AF-22
`exact_candidate_qualification` application reduces the complete vector. Release
snapshot and prior acceptance remain consumers/output evidence, never gate inputs.
