One authoritative `ExactCandidateQualification<K>` defines addressable `basis` and `verdict` projections. Subject kind is exactly `pre_rc_candidate | installed_rc`. The basis retains exact source inventory, Product/manifest/artifact/toolchain, native install/W, tenant manifest, QualificationLawBasis and frozen gate inventory. Its prospective release coordinate implements accepted D6 namespace/profile/subtree/version-line/ordinal/claim-byte law. It excludes future verdicts, its output snapshot and future human acceptance. A development version lacking that prospective identity remains non-green.

The following domain carriers have strict raw guards, constructive functions, native types and generated schemas from one owner. Canonical identities exclude their own identity fields; byte digests, typed-value digests and ABG result-body digests remain distinct.

## 3. One native proof owner

`abg/qualification_proof.ts` owns the qualification-specific native join. It exposes closed projections for the current consumer basis, admitted J/O, owning-gate evidence and selected AF22 verdict. Validator owns their domain meaning. This module delegates native truth to existing prefix, invocation, execution, CCall, actor-evidence, F_H and declaration owners; it is not another event store, admission authority, actor loop, universal proof interpreter or release verdict engine.

The same native functions are called at dispatch, Product result/judgment validation and historical replay. The dispatch entry requires the real current predecessor prefix and selected-no-evidence CCall supplied by the leaf owner. Replay uses the recorded exact prefix, not today's mutable workspace or an ambient Catalog. The existing F11 special occurrence passage is extended only for the declared qualification implementations; an arbitrary implementation cannot request a qualification authority tuple.

The closed proof-resource input carries the actual durable prefix, explicit evidence selections and supplied immutable Catalog/View declarations needed for historical owners. Assessment Context supplies the exact source/law/member bytes or their existing manifest-bound immutable asset preimages; byte count/digest and source-span checks bind them to the admitted task and frozen subject, not an arbitrary current path. Missing bytes or an unjoined freeze/source relation is incomplete, never an assumed identical source. Required material is retained in admitted inputs or exact immutable proof resources for a fresh process. These resources are evidence, not a current Catalog overlay, imported event store or new public read protocol.

For J or an owning gate's result, the join requires:

1. A validated native prefix with exact store identity and historical prefix coordinate. Every cited event is in that prefix and causally precedes its consumer. A preserved prefix may have a separately authorized append; a copied log is evidence bytes, not the same admitted store.
2. Actual invocation/Run, execution basis, Program, GraphFunction, materialized locus and selected CCall/fibre. Resolve supplied immutable declaration proof through the existing historical install/W/lock/Catalog/View verifier and exact Program closure. Root membership and child inherited subsets remain distinct. The historical owners must exist before the originating invocation; current broad Catalog membership supplies no missing authority.
3. The exact implementation or interaction owner and input/raw/output contract. Reuse `projectOpenedCCallCarrierAtPrefix` and `projectAdmittedCCallStateAtPrefix`; do not count raw-kind event values. Verify the result-body/value digests separately, result/judgment admission and required route/closed child or root relation. A valid negative assessment can have native result `success` and judgment `advance`: those mean the assessment completed, not that its subject passed.
4. For F_P, the actual admitted request, actor invocation/profile binding, observed artifact and closed transport/result evidence. Derive the domain wrapper from the admitted task and raw assessment. The artifact's claimed actor name, schema success, matching prompt hash, or a generic F_P output is insufficient.
5. Exact subject/law/catalog, Context members, AssetSurface, rule/version, selected surface/claim, evidence role and required scope. A narrower judgment serves only its covered pairs. Applicability and independence follow the source-bound role policy, never a caller's `independent: true` or a renamed actor label. The assessor is an actual admitted assessment occurrence with its evaluation grant. Construction provenance has two closed arms:
   - `native_construction`: resolve the actual admitted construction producer(s), their scope and actor/profile attribution; apply the required author/assessor independence relation to those native occurrences.
   - `external_construction`: bind the exact frozen candidate/source inventory to the relevant external STDO activation, attributed authors, preimages, delta and closure evidence. Native admission of this Context proves which records were supplied, not that their author labels or authority claims are true. Establish their attribution and identity correspondence from the sufficient source-grounded construction/grant record chain under the trusted-developer-desktop boundary, then compare the actual admitted assessor against that established attribution. No historical native author occurrence is required or invented. Missing, conflicting, unjoined or merely relabeled attribution blocks the dependent claim.

For insufficient external attribution, the declared `qualification_ruling` F_H request may ask the actual authorized source/Product owner to acknowledge the exact candidate and external record-set identities, attributed authors/identity correspondence and evaluation scope. Its closed response is `acknowledged | disputed | insufficient`, with reasons and evidence. Reuse the exact native response/actor/capability relation below; the current responding owner must occupy the declared authority slot. `acknowledged` records that present attribution act only: it does not turn external construction into past ABG events, retroauthorize it, waive independence or assert that qualification passed. The required independence comparison still applies to the established identities; disagreement or insufficient evidence remains blocked. Sufficient existing attribution needs no extra O, and no universal acknowledgment prerequisite is introduced.

For O, reuse `projectFhContinuations` and its exact Public response operation/capability join. Authenticate the request's CCall, response contract, actual responding actor/grant, response bytes, scope and basis at its historical frontier. `responded` or `resolved` may establish the typed act when its declared contract permits; an open, abandoned, superseded or conflicting continuation cannot. Do not require a fabricated successful Run to recognize an actual human response. Possessing a generic response capability does not extend the declared Product-authority slot. O can accept lawful re-entry or a justified bounded exclusion where law permits; it cannot discharge an unsatisfied mandatory obligation.

Selection is explicit native provenance, not chronology or Program-wide J counting. The admitted task/Program fixes each key before the producing effect:

| Selection family | Key and eligible-producer cardinality |
|---|---|
| J | Exact subject basis and law, admitted assessment-task identity/digest, declared assessment or independent-peer slot, role binding and exact covered rule/surface/criterion set; bind Program/locus and task ordinal/source position where the declared composition distinguishes occurrences. Require one eligible actual producer per required slot. Distinct partitions and declared independent-peer slots legitimately produce several Js in one Program, including peers assessing the same criteria. Their finite coverage and independence obligations remain separate. |
| O | Exact basis/law, F_H request/continuation identity and digest, and declared decision slot/scope. Require the actual eligible response for that request; distinct scoped requests are not competing rulings merely because they share a Run. |
| AF22 | Exact basis/law and admitted qualification episode/Program's sole declared reducer slot. Require one eligible actual AF22 producer for that episode; assessment-slot multiplicity does not multiply verdict authority. |

Resolve each result reference through the exact native producing CCall. Parent foldbacks are aliases only when the existing child/result/route relations join them to that producer. Several authenticated wrappers of one producer count once. Two distinct eligible actual producers for the same immutable selection key refuse even with identical values. A result/CCall ID, arbitrary new task label, after-the-fact coverage narrowing or role/peer renaming cannot split a predeclared slot to launder competition. A lawful native retry/supersession relation determines eligibility without erasing prior failures; first/last selection or value deduplication does not. Other historical episodes are not silently made current by choosing a result ref; their reuse remains explicitly bound by admitted selection and the owning applicability relation.

Currentness is exact-basis and owner-specific, not “most recent reviewer.” Reuse an earlier admitted J/O when subject bytes, law, input sources, role, scope, authority and required independence still hold and no native correction/supersession invalidates it at the consumer frontier. A role/context switch alone does not discard valid evidence. Changed subject/law or an affected owner invalidator blocks dependent reuse; unchanged hashes alone do not establish its admission. The selection retains earlier failures and re-entry pressure. No projection upgrades a failed command, transport failure or partial construction into successful evidence.

## 4. F11 finite evaluation and semantic responsibility

Preserve and complete the existing finite checks. Catalog source locators/spans must bind the actual Definition-selected law and published manifest asset. Inventory coordinates must bind exact immutable subject bytes and their selected roots; duplicates, undeclared members, missing declared members and crossed Product/install/W/tenant bases refuse. Catalog fidelity examines the selected governing authority population, not merely the subset the catalog author chose to list. A frozen candidate catalog's row count and source population do not establish completeness. Regenerated catalog bytes change their published identity.

The finite rule/surface matrix records every catalog rule and every applicable subject member through explicit classified coverage. For each pair, native C checks presence, uniqueness, exact coordinates, role compatibility and evidence scope. J establishes meaning, sufficiency and justified applicability/inapplicability. Unknown applicability is not omitted. Grouped J may cover an explicit set; a catalog-wide green flag cannot. A source-grounded coverage/classification assessment plus the mechanical inventory comparison addresses omitted applicable surfaces; filenames and `complete_claim` do not.

Tenant conformance checks exact capability graph/Public catalog identities, unique declared claims, owned capability/contract references and claim/evidence coverage. Existing raw/whole-Program/conformance owners compute their relations. Actual realization and proof adequacy remain the declared semantic responsibility; export presence, passing a schema or a contract count cannot establish them. The complete 18/56 export surface remains a conservation check, not the F11 verdict.

An assessment-required finding is discharged only by its corresponding
successfully resolved, adequate assessment; never by removing the check.
Result findings retain source/rule/surface, native evidence, C/J/O provenance,
diagnostics and residuals. Aggregate `failed` if any applicable obligation is
falsified or a required identity relation fails; otherwise `blocked_incomplete`
if any required evidence/applicability is missing, indeterminate or accepted
for re-entry but unsatisfied; otherwise `passed` only when the complete
applicable matrix is discharged. Justified inapplicability is limited by its
actual law and scope. F11 as a whole is not excludable.

F11 output remains `qualificationVerdict: false`. Native completion of a non-green F11 evaluation is a truthful completed result. Publication or catalog generation does not require F11 already green; the candidate's immutable inputs are frozen first and evaluated next. Evidence, reviews and verdicts produced by that evaluation are external records, not recursive inputs that must certify themselves. Preexisting proof/qualification/release-claim bytes within the candidate remain evaluated subject members.

## 5. D5 complete vector and sole AF22

The frozen gate inventory is source-derived from QUAL-064 and the applicable subject kind, not a caller-selectable subset. Each ordered row names its obligation, exact owning assessment contract/implementation and allowed typed projection relation, evidence roles and applicability. It includes root, all retained traversal rows, separate fibre substitution, complete C/malformed GTL/F_P, One Surface, F11, Consensus, native/Codex and every selected S01/S02/S03/S05/S06 obligation, including the original full mandatory application outcomes. Installed-RC reuse has the owning gate's explicit same-subject/applicability justification; acceptance introduces no third subject.

`constructQualificationGateResultVector` is a deterministic constructor over resolved native owning results. It neither executes a gate nor interprets its meaning. For F11 only, the declared projection maps `passed -> green`, `failed -> red`, `blocked_incomplete -> blocked` with unchanged basis/law, findings, evidence and bypasses. Other gates keep their own published mapping and semantic authority. A missing owning gate mechanism is a dependency, not permission for D5 to translate a test log or reviewer post into a green row. A native gate may truthfully emit blocked for missing prerequisites; a wholly missing vector row is not silently manufactured as blocked.

Whole input admission checks the exact basis/law/inventory and nonempty complete vector: zero-based contiguous order, unique gate identities, exact roster, typed dispositions, actual owning assessment/evidence refs and digests, explicit bypasses and canonical inventory/vector identity. Before consumption the native owner resolves every row at the frozen predecessor prefix. A structurally valid caller vector has no independent authority. Missing/foreign/stale/unadmitted or ambiguous backing evidence yields typed refusal without a qualification verdict.

One declared qualification Program contains exactly one result-bearing `C.of(AF22)` for `exact_candidate_qualification`. Its selected invocation/Run/Program/locus is the qualification episode used by the native proof selector in section 3. It consumes that admitted vector and emits the sole `ExactCandidateQualification<verdict>`; no qualification HOF, parallel reducer, shell controller or release harness computes another verdict. Within the complete valid vector: any red yields red; otherwise any blocked yields blocked; otherwise green. Preserve the union of all bypass refs and every gate citation. Only green with the empty bypass set is release-eligible. A valid red/blocked verdict is an admitted qualification result, not a runtime success claim about the Product.

The verdict includes the exact basis/law, inventory and vector identities, selected native producer and complete ordered citations. The native projector additionally requires its actual CCall/result/judgment and terminal qualification scope. R10's installed typed terminal projection exposes it through the existing Run/GraphCall result and replay paths; no CLI-side reader is introduced. D6 imports this same `projectQualificationVerdict` owner and schema, verifies the pre-RC or installed-RC subject it needs, and checks green/empty bypass without recomputing gate meaning or the vector. Actual human RC acceptance remains D6's separate F_H relation.

## 7. Finite discriminators and proof boundary

Every negative has a valid positive in the same claimed scope; an earlier blocked baseline gives no assurance credit to downstream mutations. Checks bind exact fixture and source/package/installed identity, native prefix, actor/profile, input/output contracts and oracle.

| Discriminator | Required observation |
|---|---|
| Legal bounded J/O -> F11 -> AF22 | Actual declared qualified assessment(s), any applicable actual F_H ruling, and same-basis finite checks produce their real native results; a complete genuinely green vector yields exactly one native green/empty-bypass verdict and fresh typed result/replay equality. A smaller mechanism fixture is labeled as such, not full current-candidate qualification. |
| Seven F11 seeds | On separately identified exact mutant subjects: missing authority, broken traceability, unowned Public contract, design/code drift, malformed proof, ticket/closure mismatch, release identity mismatch each causes its owning source-linked non-green F11 finding and prevents release-eligible AF22.
