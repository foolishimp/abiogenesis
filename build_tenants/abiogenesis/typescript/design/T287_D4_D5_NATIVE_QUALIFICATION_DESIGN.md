### Configured assessor identity and pre-invocation eligibility (Q23)

This bounded re-entry gives section 2's role binding and step 5's independence relation their actual actor fact. It is configured selection under the trusted-desktop boundary, not remote provider attestation.

1. **Identity.** `transport_contracts.ts` defines the one `ConfiguredActorIdentity`: `established` with the effective `model` and its `transport_argument` coordinate, or `unknown` with a closed reason. `deriveConfiguredActorIdentity` reads the final composed arguments under a closed per-agent grammar. Exactly one selector (`claude --model`; `codex --model | -m`) establishes the model. No selector, several selectors, an empty or option-like value, or a displacing option (`claude --fallback-model --agent --agents --settings`; `codex --profile -p --config -c --oss`) is unknown. An argv prompt is data. The grammar also declares the arity of the options the transport and actor owners compose. The next argument after a value-bearing option (`claude --output-format --permission-mode --tools --json-schema --append-system-prompt`; `codex --sandbox -o`) is data even when it is selector-shaped, so `--append-system-prompt --model=claude-opus-5-5` selects no model. A no-value flag (`claude -p --disable-slash-commands --no-session-persistence --include-partial-messages --verbose --safe-mode`; `codex --full-auto --skip-git-repo-check`) consumes nothing. A selector-shaped argument directly after an option outside the grammar, or anywhere after `--`, has no known role and is `model_selection_ambiguous`. For Claude, a non-empty sanitized `ANTHROPIC_MODEL` that differs from the selection is `model_environment_conflict`. Agents without a declared grammar are `agent_model_selection_unsupported`. No name, prefix or role is interpreted.
2. **One owned selection.** `prepareWorkerTransport` freezes its own copies of contract, schema, appended arguments and captured environment, and derives the identity from exactly those final arguments and the sanitized environment the process receives. `runPreparedWorkerTransport` executes that retained request and refuses any differing command or arguments. Later caller or ambient changes cannot change the executed selection or its identity.
3. **One binding.** `actor_process.ts` places the plan's identity once in the existing `actor_transport_binding_admitted` body as `configuredIdentity`; it dispatches only on a store on the current event profile. Recorded L and RC6 bindings carry none. `event_store.ts` allows the member only in the S2 and W binding payload variants (`configuredIdentityBindingVariants`) and refuses a recorded identity that does not re-derive from the binding's own `agentKey`, `promptTransport` and `args`. Only an environment conflict is not replayable from the binding; it is admitted only where the arguments themselves select. A binding without the member reads as `identity: null`, never as established. Adding the member changes the current event-contract profile descriptor and its digest.
4. **One projection.** `c_call.ts` `projectTransportBindingSelection` joins the binding body digest and ref, its CCall and the unique actor start it causes, then returns `{agentKey, identity}`. The existing `projectAdmittedActorTransport` uses that join and exposes `configuredSelection`. Qualification reads it through `projectAdmittedTransportEvidenceSelection`. J, Result lineage, native provenance producers and CCall carry only their existing invocation and binding coordinates. No identity body is copied.
5. **Policy meaning.** `validator/qualification.ts` resolves a selection to a declared profile only when transport agent and effective model equal exactly one `actorProfiles` row. Otherwise the selection is `identity_absent`, `identity_unknown`, `profile_unlisted` or `profile_ambiguous`. `decideQualificationDispatchEligibility` is the one decision over facts the native owner joined: the assessor selection; the authenticated native construction authors with their own binding selections; and, for `author_and_peer_distinct`, every declared peer slot with the selections of its admitted occurrences. Declared peer slots are the other slots of the same plan that share a coverage criterion, and each is required. The order is fixed:
   - `not_required` returns `not_required`.
   - An unresolved assessor is `unknown`.
   - External construction is `unknown(external_attribution_requires_future_judgment)`, because its present grounding comes only from the J being dispatched. A prior acknowledged owner ruling is not joined here; QR02 retains that.
   - Unresolved native authorship is `unknown`.
   - The assessor as author, or the same declared family as an author or peer, is `ineligible`.
   - Any unresolved author or peer profile is `unknown`.
   - A peer-distinct role is `unknown(required_peer_missing)` when its plan declares no peer slot or when any declared peer slot has no admitted occurrence. The admitted occurrences of other slots are still compared first, so a known same-family peer remains `ineligible`. Missing peer evidence is never an empty comparison.
   - Otherwise the result is `eligible`. That only permits dispatch; it never establishes independence or acceptance.
6. **Call-bound gate.** `qualification_proof.ts` `qualificationAssessorEligibility` mints one operation per admitted call and records it in an owner WeakSet. `leaf_invocation_port.ts` binds it for an assessment call only when the call basis, consumer owner and admitted catalogs exist. The operation re-checks the request's CCall, input digest, predecessor prefix and role actor. It reauthenticates the selected-no-evidence consumer owner at that prefix, then applies the decision. `actor_process.ts` applies it to the prepared plan's selection after transport preparation and before the binding/start transaction. Several cases refuse with the existing `actor_process_invocation_refused` and no admitted event or provider start: a missing operation, which covers standalone or cold `invokeActorProcess` and any caller-made function; an operation from another owner; and any decision other than `eligible`. Non-assessment implementations are not gated, and their behavior is unchanged. The operation collects admitted peer occurrences per declared peer slot at its own dispatch prefix: each actual same-Program assessment start for that slot of the same plan. A declared slot with no such start, including one whose call is open without a start, is missing, whether it is the only peer slot or one of several. A started peer without admitted transport evidence is unknown. No future peer identity, J or occurrence is inferred. Consequently a fresh peer-required plan has no dispatchable first slot until a separately selected owner relation supplies prior authenticated peer evidence; this gate refuses it, which is not a production peer route.
7. **Replay.** `resolveQualificationAssessments` applies the same decision again over the admitted bindings of each selected J, the native authors and the selected peer J of every declared peer slot. Any decision other than `eligible` blocks the resolution. A legacy binding, an unlisted, mismatched, substituted, crossed or digest-changed binding, or the same family blocks it warm and cold.

Production limits: the specialized native actor route constructs only the `claude` transport. Native authors and assessors that pass through it share the declared Anthropic Claude family, so actual production positive eligibility needs a separately selected eligible route and sufficient prior attribution. A peer-required plan additionally needs that separate relation for its peer evidence. The identity reflects configured arguments and environment under the closed grammar above. It does not observe provider-side routing, aliases resolved by the provider or subagent models. An option outside the grammar is assumed to consume at most the next argument; a selector-shaped argument in that position is unknown, and one later is read as a selector.

Currentness is exact-basis and owner-specific, not “most recent reviewer.” Reuse an earlier admitted J/O when subject bytes, law, input sources, role, scope, authority and required independence still hold and no native correction/supersession invalidates it at the consumer frontier.
