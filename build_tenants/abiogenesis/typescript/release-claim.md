## What the cut contains

The TypeScript tenant realizes the fifteen selected ABIogenesis 5.0 families
`A5-F01` to `A5-F07`, `A5-F09` to `A5-F11` and `A5-F13` to `A5-F17` as the
Product definition states them: one exact product, install, workspace and
catalog; complete GTL authoring and validation; complete graph, C and
traversal execution with the 4.6 traversal conservation contract; probabilistic
result integrity; one public contract authority; the thin public SDK and CLI;
the complete One Surface loop; catalog semantics; event-sourced runtime truth;
self-conformance; native independence; the installed language and runtime
mechanics proof; exact-candidate qualification; the immutable candidate and
accepted release path; and the specification-driven downstream lifecycle.

The packaged default library carries the ordinary Executive, governance and
lifecycle graphs through the same publication path as every other module. No
downstream catalog product is a dependency of this cut.

The sandbox acceptance workloads are post-deploy tests of the installed
product, not features. On this candidate's pack, six of the seven Hello
workloads (basic-cli, js-tenant-test, js-sdlc-bootstrap, rust-cli,
rust-service, parallel-js) produced, corrected and closed a working
application with independent assessment and fresh readback. The Data Mapper
workload runs at the installed-candidate qualification.

## What the cut does not claim

- Production of `yield_continuation` is reserved for 5.1 under Product Release
  Boundaries. Its conserved type and dormant branches remain; other admitted
  continuation, restart and recovery promises retain their 5.0 applicability.

- `A5-F08`, standalone Consensus free-construction qualification, and
  `A5-F12`, the dedicated replay-grounded observer and tuner Product, are
  reserved 5.1 outcomes. The native human-response portions of F03, F06, F07
  and F10, host parity and Codex parity are likewise reserved.
- Dynamic graph generation and recomposition at runtime is excluded. Candidate
  definitions cross publication, validation and admission before execution.
  Whether the observer and tuner Product and runtime graph generation are one
  mechanism is a product question for the next intake.
- The downstream catalog product `odd_glc` is not completed by this release;
  its catalog role is covered by the packaged default library. Its retirement
  is a goal and product question for the next intake.
- Three owner questions of the release resumption (15, 16 and 17) are open and
  non-blocking; none changes a contract of this cut.
