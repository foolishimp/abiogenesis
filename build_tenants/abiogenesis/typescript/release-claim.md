## Delta from 5.0.0-rc.1

1. **Truthful C2 configuration diagnostics.** `constructWorksiteCommandConfiguration`
   refuses a duplicate `commandId`, and a protected subject inside an allowed
   write territory, through separate guards whose messages name the offending
   identity or path. Both still throw `TypeError`. The remaining conditions
   keep their combined guard and message; no guard is removed or loosened. The
   C2 renderer context pin carries the recompiled module's member digest, byte
   count and span offsets; its span digest and policy text are unchanged.
2. **Early assessment role separation at intake.** `isGovernanceWorkState`, and
   so `constructGovernanceWorkState`, refuses an assessment selection whose
   sources repeat, include `candidatePath` or `rubricPath`, or whose
   `candidatePath` equals `rubricPath`. This is the existing native assessment
   rule applied at governance intake. It reuses the existing governance-input
   `TypeError`; the native check, schemas and contract shapes are unchanged.
   Retained governance states with overlapping roles now fail that predicate;
   their recovery is a corrected new input.
3. **Owner-fixture role repair.** Two owner-test fixtures each change one
   role-selection line, plus one comment line, so source, candidate and rubric
   are disjoint. Assertions, owner calls, counts, timeouts and skips are
   unchanged.
4. **Identity.** Package, lock root and Product constants move from
   `5.0.0-rc.1` to `5.0.0-rc.2`. Dependency versions and integrity entries are
   unchanged.
